## Principal’s Report for School Council

Wednesday, September 20th, 2023  
School  
We’ve had a very smooth start up to the 2023-2024 school year. We have welcomed 35 new JK  
students, along with about 40 other new students in other grades. We have a total of 18  
classes (last year we had 19) and our population currently sits at 397.  
We’ve also welcomed a handful of new staff members (teachers Mrs. Barlow, Ms. Duquette,  
Ms. Sullivan and Mme Wu, EA Mrs. Avci and custodian Mrs. Nash). We’re lucky to say  
“welcome back” to teachers Mrs. Skilling, Mr. Moffett and Mrs. Cressman, along with EA Ms.  
Brule.  
The City of Waterloo has crossing guards in place at both Erbsville and Laurelwood and again at  
Laurelwood and American Beech, which we are so grateful for to help keep our students and  
families safe.  
We had a fun “welcome back” assembly on the first Friday of school in the gym where we  
practiced singing our school song, practiced our new cheer and then went out on the back field  
for a huge scavenger hunt. The kids had a blast!  
We are excited to have motivational speaker QMack coming to us on Friday, September 22nd  
and all our staff and students will get to enjoy his “Be and All-Star” show. QMack is very  
entertaining and uses basketball tricks to reinforce character attributes like respect, attitude,  
leadership, love, self-control and teamwork for students.  
We have changed our daily schedule slightly this year. Families won’t notice much, if any,  
changes at all as our start and end times remain the same. However, we have adjusted the  
time of our second nutrition break from 1:20 to 1:50 p.m. This means that we now have 2 “big”  
instruction blocks of 4 periods at the beginning and in the middle of the day and only a 2-period  
block at the end of the day after second NB. This give teachers more “double blocks” of  
instruction and maximizes the instructional time when students are at their freshest and able to  
do their best learning (morning and middle blocks).  
We have some new furniture in some common areas of our school. You may have noticed at  
our Meet the Staff night last week that we have created a “lounge” area in our main foyer.  
Upstairs we have created a “learning commons” in our choir loft. Many of our junior students  
use this space for group work and they now have some couches, tables and plants in their  
space.

We have taken advantage of the nice weather and have practiced our fire drills and also a  
lockdown drill so far this year. The students at Abraham Erb are so cooperative and take these  
drills very seriously. They do a fantastic job.  
We are going to continue our partnership with Panago Pizza this year and offer pizza days on  
Fridays. The ordering information is out on School Day.  
We are excited to share with you that we are going to be hatching chicks this year. The school  
will be buying a starter kit (incubator, heat lamp, cage, food, fertilized eggs, bedding, etc) and  
we will be running this out of our library. The students will be able to watch the entire process  
from fertilization to hatching and they’re sure to be enthralled. This project will be under the  
leadership of our head secretary Mrs. Balkos, who has done this many times and is very  
experienced.  
Board  
As you are all aware, the WRDSB started this school year with a PD Day on Tuesday, September  
5th. This allowed all staff to complete a full day of mandatory training (which historically has  
happened on PD Days in October/November).  
The WRDSB now has opened registration for the International and Indigenous Languages  
programs, which are held on Saturdays. Classes begin on September 30th and finish on June 1st

Province  
Many school staff (including teachers, EAs, secretaries, custodians) having been working  
without a contract since August of 2022. Last year, there wasn’t much movement or progress  
with bargaining. Recently, OSSTF, representing secondary school teachers and a few other  
employee groups, announced that it was going to ask members to vote on a process called  
binding arbitration this fall. Our elementary teachers’ union, ETFO, has rejected binding  
arbitration and has asked for a conciliator to help with negotiations. ETFO will also be holding a  
strike vote this fall, allowing the bargaining process to move to next steps should an agreement  
not be reached with continued negotiations with a conciliator.  
EQAO results should be available soon. Individual reports for all students who wrote the test  
last year will be sent home with them as soon as we get them.
