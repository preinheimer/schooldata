## Teachers’ Report for School Council

Wednesday, October 16th, 2024  
Here is the latest for October for Abraham Erb:  
This month in Ms Sullivan and Mme Morrison’s Grade 2 class, we have been busy making  
simple machines for our science unit. We made Pom Pom catapults from popsicle sticks and  
elastics while learning about Levers. The students worked well together experimenting with the  
fulcrum and trying out different things to make their Pom Pom launch the farthest! Next we  
will explore inclined planes by building ramps and racing cars. Looking forward to  
experimenting, creating, and engineering with our Grade 2’s this month as we explore the 6  
simple machines.  
This month, Mme McAllister, Mme Zehr, Ms. Fox, and Ms. Skilling’s classes had a wonderful  
field trip to Landmark Cinemas to watch The Wild Robot. After enjoying the book at school,  
students were thrilled to see the story come to life on the big screen. This experience deepened  
their engagement with our Language curriculum by enhancing comprehension and critical  
thinking as they compared the book and the movie. Additionally, it supported our Science  
studies by illustrating concepts such as animal habitats, adaptations, and the impact of  
technology on nature.  
Mrs. Barlow’s grade 1/2 class has started to use the chromebooks. Normally this would not be  
a big deal, but for grade one and two students it actually is! Students at this age take a little (or  
a lot!) of time to learn to login independently. Eventually they will learn how to navigate  
Google Classroom, use Google as a research tool, listen to stories read aloud through  
Tumblebooks, and they will work on their math skills using board approved programs like the  
new Reflex Math program.  
Page 5 of 10  
In Mme Elkadri’s grade 3 social studies class, they have been learning about the Anishnabee  
community through role-playing activities, and the kids have been having a great time engaging  
with the lessons in this fun and interactive way. Also in Mme Elkadri’s grade 5/6 class, the focus  
has been on oral communication in French, and the students have been doing an excellent job  
speaking to her in French with growing confidence!  
Mme McAllister's Grade 3 French Immersion class recently enjoyed a wonderful visit from the  
University of Waterloo's Let's Talk Science program. The students participated in an engaging  
and interactive workshop exploring the remarkable diversity of plants. They learned about the  
various adaptations in common food plants and how these adaptations help plants survive in  
different environments.  
The members of running club have been spending their Wednesday afternoons working on  
their running skills at second nutrition break. We had 4 students represent us at the Lester B  
Pearson Fun Run after school on October 16. Well done, runners!  
The Grade 1 students will be headed to Bejamin Tree farm for a pumpkin exploration on the  
31st! We have lots of pumpkin learning planned!

## Principal’s Report for School Council

Wednesday, October 16th, 2024  
We had a great speaker, Unstoppable Tracy, on September 27th. Tracy is a 4-way amputee  
and talked to our students about her experiences as a child in school and never giving up. She  
Page 6 of 10  
was a very engaging speaker and our students learned a lot from her about perseverance and  
the importance of not leaving anyone behind.  
Our annual Terry Fox Run was held on Thursday, September 26th. The weather was awesome,  
and the students had a lot of fun running/walking and collecting beads for their “fox” bracelets.  
Thanks to the generous support of our families, Abe Erb raised \$691.40 for cancer research.  
Over the past 18 years in hosting this event, we’ve raised over \$11 500 for cancer research,  
which is wonderful.  
The City of Waterloo is going to be building a splash pad in the area between the city  
playground and the high school. Construction is due to begin sometime this fall and continue  
in the spring, with the hope that the splash pad will be operational come the warmer weather  
in 2025. Keep your eyes open for this!  
I’d like to give a shout-out to Taru Malhotra, who ran a neighbourhood group for girls this past  
summer, encouraging them to explore various STEM activities. As part of this, they did some  
bake-sale fundraising and purchased some STEM kits for each of our grade 3 classes here at  
school. These materials were so appreciated by the grade 3 classes who received them and  
will be well-utilized for years to come.  
We are having a “communication coreboard” installed outside on the brick wall by our  
kindergarten and primary classrooms. This board is meant to be of help to students who have  
limited verbal abilities, or to those whose first language isn’t English in communicating their  
needs. For example, a student can point to the appropriate picture to indicate that they are  
hurt or that they would like to change to another activity or use the washroom. We are very  
excited to see the positive difference this communication tool will make for many of our  
students.

We are excited to share that we will be hosting a Winter Variety Show again this year. Classes  
will be working together, and all students will have the opportunity to perform. We will be  
having one show on Thursday, December 19th at 12:15 p.m. to give families the chance to come  
and see the performance. A “save the date” announcement was sent out on School Day and  
more reminders will follow as we get closer to the date.  
I am going to incorporate my “school” updates with Board and Provincial updates and give a  
summary of Spring 2024 EQAO results. The results of the 2024 EQAO testing were made public  
on September 26th. This was our third year of having grade 3s and grade 6s participate in the  
new digital version of the testing. Anyone who is interested can access results from all  
schools in Ontario through the EQAO website.  
Provincially, both grades 3 and 6 Reading and Writing results are down slightly from last year,  
however both are up slightly in math. In the WRDSB, grade 3 scores in Reading, Writing and  
Math are down as are Reading and Writing results in grade 6. Math in grade 6 is up a slight  
amount. I created a one-page summary of our results from Abraham Erb and it will be  
included in the minutes from this meeting. We are pleased that our students continue to  
perform very well in Reading, Writing and Math, significantly surpassing both the Board and the  
province in our results. Our grade 3 results in Writing and math are down a bit from last year  
but Reading is up. In grade 6, we are down a bit in all three areas, but still performing very well.  
In any given year, there can be some fluctuation in results dependent on the number of  
students who participate, the number of students with special education needs who participate  
and the number of multi-language learners who participate. We will be spending some time as  
a staff having a closer deconstructing our data in all three areas and looking at teaching next  
steps.
