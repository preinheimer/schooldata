## Principal’s Report

Discussion:  
▪ Today was the first full day in the full portable. All went smoothly.  
▪ Gratitude assembly was led by Mrs. Wentworth this month.  
▪ Mr. Maloney and Mrs. Watson will be sharing mindfulness practices for next assembly (Nov 26),  
calming bin activities will be shared for the month in classrooms.  
▪ Progress reports and interviews were completed last week.  
▪ We had a moving remembrance day assembly; a Veteran came in to do teachings with grade 5/6,  
great messages of peace offered through a personal connection, choir sang, artwork from classes  
was shared.  
▪ Donations for food drive were received, a big thank you from HS. Continuing to get warm winter  
clothing in for the drive.  
▪ School board has approved moving phase 2 of the boundary study earlier from 2020 and will  
happen now instead. This means that we are looking at where our grade 6s will transition for  
grade 7 in September 2019. Options are Vista Hills or Laurelwood. JK and CM are on the working  
group for Abraham Erb starting in December and there will be opportunities for feedback.  
Decision will be made by spring for September.  
▪ In discussions and working with constable David Chen as well as community, bylaw officers for  
parking issues, parking resource office to work on issues with parking in fire route.  
▪ David Chen making connections with students in a positive way and will be back in December to  
do a presentation on being a good citizen on the internet.  
▪ Trips this month: outdoor ed, music concert, safety village.  
▪ Jr volleyball club wrapping up and intramurals on‐going.  
▪ SK parents received info on french immersion nights in November. Registration info coming for  
the New Year.  
▪ Human Society will be visiting classes Dec 10 & 11  
▪ Winter Variety Show is Dec 20 at 12pm.  
▪ Little jammers will be returning for the ukulele program, starting in January, once a week during  
the breaks.  
▪ Mad science registration will be starting Jan 21.  
▪ This year the school will partnering with stem robotics to offer nutrition break programs for grade  
1‐3.
