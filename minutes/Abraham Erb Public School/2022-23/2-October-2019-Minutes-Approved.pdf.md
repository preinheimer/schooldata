## 2019/2020 Planning Session

Discussion:  
▪ JK described the purpose of this session to develop a list of approved purchases and events that  
will outline how Council will invest in the school (money and time) and how those investments  
will be funded (fundraising, grants, or cash).  
▪ PK went through extensive review with staff for funding requests from Council. The role of  
Council funds is to enrich programs or activities, not to pay for basic materials or consumables.  
▪ PK reviewed staff‐led submissions for funding.  
▪ See Appendix 3 for submissions.  
▪ JK motioned to approve \$5,550 in staff‐led submissions. VK seconded. All in favour.  
▪ JK shared info about the Libella project and different schools that have painted their washrooms  
with inspirational messages (https://www.libella.ca/schoolspainted)  
▪ MP suggested volunteers or students could paint the washrooms and perhaps hang paintings that  
can be changed so the art won’t be overlooked or become stagnant. Others agreed and  
elaborated on this idea, citing ME to WE.  
▪ JK motioned to approve a subcommittee to investigate inspirational wall paintings for \$2500. AP  
seconded. All were in favour.  
▪ CM discussed STEAM night event and how it was run last year as an arts and science night with a  
painting activity and Engineering Science Quest from UW.  
▪ JK motioned to approve \$1500 in funds for STEAM night. SB seconded. All in favour.  
▪ JK discussed committees needed for events and activities throughout the year:  
o Family STEAM night  
o Family Fun Night (May 28, likely)  
o Spirit Wear (reopen sales for school branded clothing)  
o 2 or 3 Parent Reps for Eco Club (specifically to look at school greening)
