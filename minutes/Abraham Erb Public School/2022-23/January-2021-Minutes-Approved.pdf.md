replicate what teachers do in the classroom. Whatever parents can do is deeply appreciated.

▪ Wish:  
o Synchronize schedules across classrooms and schools. This will help parents better  
manage time for lunches, breaks, and outside when there are multiple children at home.  
o Please don’t shuffle classes as the relationships are established with teachers and friends.  
o Add more structure in the day so kids and parents know what teacher will be on next.  
o Getting EA support has been difficult. Need more support for kids with special needs.  
o Provide a more consistent workload between classes. One kid has lots to keep busy and  
engaged, and one barely has 30 minutes of work a day.  
o Kids need clear expectations. Unable to ask questions to the teacher. Kids are working  
much more than necessary.

## Parents Reaching Out (PRO) Grant

Presenter: Jennifer K.  
Discussion:  
▪ School councils can apply for \$500 per school for any activity to support parent engagement.  
▪ In the past, we’ve used the money for art and science night.  
▪ Application process is open and due February 5th. Must spend money by June 15th.  
▪ Considering not applying this year as we have plenty of money in the bank and no specific plans.  
As the board has a limited budget, this would leave more money available to schools that might  
not have the funds that we have available.  
▪ PK ‐ Parents do so much for teachers to show appreciation (like coffee and taco day). Could the  
council consider doing something for the parents to thank them for their contribution?  
▪ JK ‐ Pro Grant cannot be used for this purpose. Do we want to do this with our funds?  
▪ VK ‐ How many parents are there? What would the cost be? Don’t want to take funds away from  
spending on the school/kids.
