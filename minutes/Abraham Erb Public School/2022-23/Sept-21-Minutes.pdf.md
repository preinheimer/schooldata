## Teacher’s Report for School Council

Wednesday, September 21st, 2022  
Staff is happy to be back in the classroom, building their communities and looking forward to a  
positive year.  
A few of the items that are happening/have happened in the school in the month of September:  
● Last week students in grades 5 and 6 began their leadership positions ‐ which include  
office helpers, announcers, lunchroom helpers, door helpers, sign assistants, janitorial  
helpers and pizza day helpers. They are extremely excited to be involved around the  
school.  
● The Terry Fox run will happen this Friday. Laura Watson is our staff organizer and she has  
been very busy putting up posters and writing announcements. Classes are excited to  
participate on Friday.  
● Picture Day is scheduled for September 29. Class photos will also be taken again this  
year.  
● Many classes are looking to schedule field trips throughout the fall. Sadly with the bus  
interruptions it may prove difficult to secure bussing for trips. A few of the junior classes  
have already had Al Woodhouse from the Outdoor education centre out to the school to  
do some invertebrate investigations in our monarch waystation. Other classes have  
begun to explore trips/excursions within walking distance of the school.  
● In the next few weeks, our school will begin the Nutrition for Learning program. Becca  
Maxwell and Meghan Wentworth are organizing a space for the program in the school  
and assigning grade 6 students to assist during breaks.  
● Abe and Erb are getting ready to kick off our Thanksgiving Food Drive. Staff and a group  
of students will be collecting food starting Sept. 26th. Which is already this Monday! We  
will put information out on School Day, and announcements throughout next week. The  
sign outside will also be on display, so we’re hoping that this will be a great first food  
drive.

## Principal’s Report for School Council

Wednesday, September 21st, 2022  
School  
● Our September start‐up has gone really smoothly this year. We have an additional class  
(up by one from last year) and have a total of 19 classes, and 446 students. We have also  
welcomed a number of new staff members, including our new head secretary, Mrs. Jenn  
Balkos, who just started at the end of August and is doing a fantastic job in our main  
office, working alongside Mrs. Cherie Blair.  
● The new pinnies that School Council generously agreed to fund have arrived and they  
look fantastic! This will allow us to represent Abraham Erb with pride at our Regional  
Junior Track and Field meet in June and ensure that all students have a well‐fitted pinnie  
to wear.  
● Both portables on the back tarmac have been removed and delivered to schools that  
need them. This has freed up some play space on our tarmac and allows for better and  
safer “sight lines” for our staff to supervise students.  
● I have received verification that WRDSB Facilities Services will be funding the refreshing of  
all our “line games” and painting on our back tarmac. The cost is over \$2000 so it’s great  
that the Board will be picking up the tab for this. The painting will be completed soon.  
● At the end of the year, we received an additional 12 HEPA filtration units to be used  
throughout the school. Those units, along with our pre‐existing mechanical ventilation  
system allows us to have as much clean and fresh air as possible inside the school.  
● I have been having ongoing communication with the City of Waterloo around getting a  
crossing guard in place at the intersection of Laurelwood Drive and American Beech  
Avenue where many of our students and families cross to get to and from school. At this  
point, there is no guarantee, but the City is hearing my concerns and taking it to their  
September Council meeting for approval. They are actively recruiting new guards and we  
have assisted this process with a School Day blast sent out a couple of weeks ago. We  
have also sent out some messaging clarifying the parking bylaws in place along  
Laurelwood Drive. In the interim, we again have some grade 6 students, who have been  
specially trained by Student Transportation Services of Waterloo Region assisting with the  
safe crossing of students. I would encourage any parents who have concerns to directly  
contact the City of Waterloo to further advocate for the need for a crossing guard and  
perhaps even some parking bylaw reinforcement. The more voices that are heard by the  
City, the better!  
● WRDSB schools have recently been informed that some of the communication apps and  
online educational programs commonly used in many schools are no longer being  
approved from the standpoint of privacy and online security. Thus, our staff have had to  
make some pivots in terms of how they communicate with families. For example, the  
“Talking Points” app is no longer approved nor is “See Saw.” Though these apps have  
been very popular with staff and families alike, we have no choice but to adhere to the  
4  
new guidelines and stick to only using those apps and programs that have been officially  
approved by the WRDSB.  
Board  
● We are still feeling the many impacts of the July 10th “cyber incident”. Many of our  
systems are still not fully operational. For example, families can only receive  
announcements on School Day, but are not yet able to log in and complete forms,  
permissions or payments. In addition, any new families who have joined Abraham Erb  
from outside of the WRDSB are not able to create School Day accounts. There are also  
ongoing challenges with adding new students to Google classrooms and some other  
internal programs (e.g. WRDSB staff cannot log an ITS ticket). We keep putting one foot  
in front of the other and exercise patience as the Board is doing its very best to getting us  
fully operational again.  
● As of September 2022, all WRDSB‐operated Before and After School programs will  
operate as licensed childcare programs under the Child Care and Early Years Act. One of  
the biggest impacts is that by September 2025 fees will go down to an average of \$10/day  
for families using the program with children 5 years or younger.  
● The WRDSB has released its detailed Strategic Plan. Anyone who is interested is welcome  
to have a look at it on the WRDSB website.  
● As with many school boards across the province, we continue to face some significant  
bussing challenges due to the driver shortage. Student Transportation Services of  
Waterloo Region has recently announced that it will unfortunately have to cancel some  
bus runs on a rotating basis due to this shortage. Thankfully, at Abraham Erb, we have  
yet to be impacted by this.  
Province  
● All school boards across Ontario are still waiting for EQAO test results to come out  
sometime this fall. Students who wrote the test will each receive an individual student  
report.  
● All of the education unions are currently without a contract as they all expired on August  
31st. We are hoping for smooth negotiations as that process unfolds this year.
