## Student presentation

a. Chase and Ekamveer are Grade 8 students: gaga ball pit idea. The students would like to  
build a gaga ball pit in the yard. A gaga ball pit is an enclosed hexagonal area, wherein  
10 students and a ball play a version of dodgeball. The cost to build this hexagonal  
structure is about \$2350 + HST, as quoted by Fred’s Fencing who is an approved vendor  
with the Board. There are 8 schools ahead of us in line for spring construction. Students  
would like to run an outdoor carnival in April to raise money for the gaga ball pit. They  
already have some donations of gift cards as prizes during the carnival. Plan is to give  
each student one ticket for free, then additional tickets are 1 dollar each. Therefore  
each student needs to spend 3 dollars.  
b. The gaga ball pit floor would consist of woodchips – the school board would replace this  
ground cover every year. The gaga ball pit can be used in the winter. The spot for the pit  
is not yet picked – likely near the edge of the field so other games such as soccer are not  
affected.

l. They will not be allowing kids to use lockers, many grade 8 students are out in portables,  
and there isn’t enough time in transition to access the lockers anyways, and they don’t  
need them really.

## Treasurer’s report

a. No change to the amount of funds available (approximately 4K right now). b. Motion  
regarding supporting the gaga ball pit idea as presented by Heidi: i. We will use student  
council funds to fund the gaga ball pit. We will tell the students that we will match the

after the carnival, in a

## Equity work

a. How to engage more of our community school members at our school council,  
especially marginalized groups. Decided to focus on the Muslim population to start  
with, given that these parents are underrepresented at our meetings and Muslim  
students make up a significant portion of our student population.  
b. There are listening forums that the board is hosting to try to engage different groups.  
Heidi was not allowed to attend the Muslim listening group because she is not Muslim.  
Heidi will be meeting with two representatives from the Women’s Muslim Association  
instead to try and understand some of the barriers and have beginning conversation on  
how we can reduce these to promote school council engagement.
