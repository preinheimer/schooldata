Discussion and update on outdoor School Nutrition Breaks.  
Due to condition of field, tarmac only nutrition breaks are  
necessary and due to limited size of tarmac, each Grade  
only gets one time/day on tarmac; other break is inside.  
Teachers trying to get classes outside for daily physical  
activity (DPA) as much as possible. When field becomes  
harder (freezes), both nutrition breaks will again be  
outdoors.  
\-  
Due to supervision limitations, cannot get both grades out  
for 20 min t each break  
\-  
Lockers: site by site decisions. Some schools have opened  
up lockers for some grade, eg. Gr 8. Centennial has 6 x Gr  
8 classes in portables and 2 in school which would mean a  
lot of traffic in and out of school.  
\-  
School Board does hire lunch monitors; requests must be  
out in at end of June. At Centennial they have an additional  
175 minutes funded through EAs, etc.
