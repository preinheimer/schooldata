## Vice-Principal’s report

\-  
Provided overview of meeting between Heidi and Steve and Julie week prior with focus  
on how to engage members of marginalized communities in the school and School  
Council. Had agreed to focus on Muslim population as they make up a large part of our  
school. Reviewed some activities and engagement already being done within the  
school. Also plan to reach out to local Imam for their perspective and as a potential  
person of importance in the community who may be able to help us engage others. Also  
agreed to host a school open house in the spring in which School Council will also  
present and share “who we are” and “why you should become involved” with the larger  
school community including those of the Muslim faith. Heidi also shared about the  
WRDSB Listening Forums being offered for various marginalized groups, including the  
Muslim community within the WRDSB and if ok, she will attend to hear the perspectives  
and hopefully be able to bring back to this group how we can be more aware of barriers  
and possible ways to engage in a more productive manner. Harish also mentioned  
presenter available through BEP re: growing up in two cultures; Julie/Heidi/Harish will  
follow up to see if available and appropriate for a parent engagement forum and/or  
Spring Open House once planned. Discussion around barriers and accommodations,  
including language, being present as a visible minority, and creating new space where  
everyone belongs versus trying to fit people into the existing space.  
\-  
Sports and Clubs can resume as of recently. Centennial is looking at how we will do  
this. Can mix cohorts; must track attendance; identify staff who can take part and lead.  
Cohorting at outdoor breaks is still in place for now.

Staff meetings continue to focus on how to continue to work on dismantling inherent  
racism within systems
