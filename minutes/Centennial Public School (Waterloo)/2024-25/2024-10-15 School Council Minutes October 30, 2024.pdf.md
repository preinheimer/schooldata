## Principal’s Report – Steve Sherlock

● Enrolment-Spring of the previous school year, Principals submit the number of students they  
know will be at Centennial for the next school year. This number includes existing grade 7 students and  
the numbers from each feeder school. The Waterloo Region District School Board (WRDSB) uses this  
number for the number of classes and full time teachers schools will have for September.  
● WRDSB reassesses student enrollment every year in October. Due to this assessment,  
Centennial had to reduce the number of classes, staff and many reassignments of teachers were made.  
● Clubs & Sports: we are well on our way this year with many clubs, sports teams and  
intramurals. The Centennial Instagram account posts every Friday, a weekly calendar of the days  
and times for all events happening during the following week.  
https://www.instagram.com/cnw  
\_  
wrdsb/?hl=en  
● School-day. If you don’t already have an account, school-day is the best way to keep up to date  
with what is going on in the school and in your child’s class. https://www.school-day.com/  
● Nutrition for Learning is a charitable organization that supports student nutrition programs  
across Waterloo Region in all three school boards. Nutrition for Learning supplies Centennial with  
snacks that are distributed throughout the school in each class. They are currently in need of help. If  
you would like to donate, you can do so on their website: https://nutritionforlearning.ca/.  
● Vanessa Haffner Mme. Simmons and the Changemaker’s Club sold ice cream and were able to  
donate those proceeds to Nutrition for Learning.  

## School Council Initiatives

● During the previous school year it was brought to the attention of school council that  
Centennial is an old school which is in need of updates. Some projects are school-funded  
while others can be requested through the WRDSB’s Facilities department.  
● The gym refresh project (not covered by the Facilities department) was brought up  
during this meeting. The ask was made for repainting of the lines on the floor, 2 glass  
basketball backboards and painting of a Centennial Lion. Cost = \$20,000.  
● Council voted on the gym refresh and has agreed to a maximum spend of \$20,000  
minus any money that has already been donated through the Waterloo Education Foundation  
Inc. (WEFI) account. If you are interested in donating to the gym refresh, as an individual or  
a business, you can donate through the WRDSB website:  
https://www.wrdsb.ca/wefi/donate/. In the “Fund” dropdown, choose Centennial - Waterloo  
PS 0024. Please note that you will receive a tax receipt for donating to WEFI.

## Other

● Chick Hatch – Stephanie Evans  
Stephanie proposed to school council the idea of having a chick hatch in the Library this  
spring. She has 7 years’ experience running this in previous schools. The science teachers  
and librarian have expressed interest in this idea. Rental costs were approximately \$350 per  
year whereas buying the equipment would be \$650. Stephen Sherlock was interested in  
trying it for a year to see how it goes. Parent-Niveen, has offered to fund the chick hatch  
with a donation of \$650 to purchase the equipment. Stephanie will source the products and  
work with Niveen on purchasing the equipment. Stay tuned for more information to come  
and a possible webcam to be set up to watch the hatching!
