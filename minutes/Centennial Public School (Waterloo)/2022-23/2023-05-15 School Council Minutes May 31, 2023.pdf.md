## Principal’s Report – Stephen Sherlock

• We are wrapping up this year and planning for next year; we have started staffing for next year •  
The school organization looks fairly similar, but we are down 1 grade 7 class • For staffing, FTE is  
based on a full-time teacher plus planning support time  
• We were originally down three teachers, because we were down 2 classes, but we’re now only  
down by 1 class. As a result we will have 1 teacher departing (a contract teacher, who has a  
position available elsewhere). The specific teacher departing will be announced at a later date.  
• For next year we will have 7 grade 7 classes, 3 of which are French Immersion. We will also have 8  
grade 8 classes, 3 of which are French Immersion. In addition, we will continue to have our  
congregated ACE and enrichment programs.  
• Next year we will have some parental leaves, and we will hire temp teachers to cover.  
● Look for information about staffing changes in August/September.  
• We have one retirement to announce. Educational assistant Michelle Craddock, who has been at  
Centennial for 17 years. She will be very missed!
