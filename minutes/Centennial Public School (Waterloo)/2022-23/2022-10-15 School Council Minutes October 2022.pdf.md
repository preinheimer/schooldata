## Pizza Day Update - Heidi Holmes

● One of the major fundraisers for the school this year will be Pizza days, which will run on  
Thursdays starting Thursday October 27th.  
● Because school day was offline at the beginning of the year, it delayed the start of pizza  
days.  
● A survey of topping choices was developed by Stephen Sherlock and filled out by  
students. They were asked to vote on topping options from the supplier. This included  
cheese, pepperoni, halal pepperoni, GF crust, and veggie. Very few wanted GF or halal  
options, so at this time we will not be offering either. We considered that cheese and  
veggie pizza would accommodate students who wanted halal options.  
● Cost is about \$10 per pizza, but GF was \$15 and without enough interest, we ruled it out  
since the primary purpose of pizza days is to act as a fundraiser.  
● The supplier (Double Double Pizza) was chosen for proximity (Amos and Erb). In  
addition, they were able to accommodate our days, and are providing paper plates and  
napkins.  
● Students order pizza one week in advance. Heidi summarizes all of the orders each  
week and forwards it to the pizza vendor.  
● Pizzas will be delivered at around 10 in the morning, for first break at 10:15.  
● During the first order period, we had 200 pizza orders totaling 347 slices. Some of the  
orders were for students ordering more than one type of pizza. About 40% of students  
participated in the first Pizza Day.  
● Pizza is \$2.50 per slice. From that cost, 10 cents from each slice goes toward school day  
costs. We make about \$1.40 per slice profit. For the first week with 347 slices ordered, it  
should be approximately \$485 profit from this one week.  
● A parent volunteer will oversee receipt and distribution of the pizza. In addition, class  
reps will distribute the pizzas to the right classrooms. Teachers will have a list of who  
ordered what type, and how many slices.  
● It will be easier to “block order” going forward, meaning one order per student for several  
weeks of Pizza days. We will probably start ordering in this way in January. It will be  
approximately 6-8 weeks at a time.

Carizon Centre Information - Jordan Wettlaufer  
● The Centre is called Erb West Care and Share – located at 45 Amos Street  
○  
Monday-Thursday 1 pm - 4:30 pm

accessible any time the Centre is open (Monday-Thursday 1 pm - 4:30 pm).

non-partisan.
