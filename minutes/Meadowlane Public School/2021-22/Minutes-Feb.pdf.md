Teachers Rep – Stephanie Webb  
Talked to Lexia Pilot program – showed us a slide show to show us how it supports the children and the  
staff  
Computer program that aims to build the foundational reading skills for students  
Science or 6 pillars of reading – phonological awareness, phonics, fluency, vocabulary, comprehension,  
and structural analysis  
Students get certificates based on their achievement  
Helps teachers with the tools for a wide range of student’s levels  
Students are given a placement test when they first start to ensure they are started at the right level for  
their success  
Went through examples of what the students learn – the scope and sequence  
Showed difference between kindergarten to grade 5 and how the lessons change  
Teachers are seeing great growth from the beginning in October till now – students are referring things  
to learning it in Lexia  
Because it’s a pilot project, teachers have been reporting back – the board is then looking at results to  
see if it’s a good program to keep at Meadowlane and also introduce to other schools  
Dreambox is another program/tool that is used for mathematical learning  
Online learning platform like lexia, with over 2000 math lessons – aligned with our math curriculum –  
understanding number sense and problem solving  
It adapts to the students learning no matter what level they are at  
Students are assigned their grade level and or can be changed on the their level of knowledge  
Performance is sent to teachers so teachers know where they are at and if intervention need to take  
place  
60-90 minutes a week to support their learning  
Teachers can see all lessons covered and students milestone – can see where they are doing great or  
having trouble  
Teachers can assign lessons to students – as well as quizzes or assessments – students think they are just  
fun math games but it’s giving teachers a look at where student is
