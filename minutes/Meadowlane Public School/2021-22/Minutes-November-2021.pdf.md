Lexia pilot project – online reading program – literacy – Board picked us a pilot school – adaptive  
program – lots of resources for staff – assessment pieces so teachers can see and identify gaps  
in kids learning  
December meeting – would like to have Stephanie Webb on the meeting to talk more in depth  
about the program  
Teacher Rep – Greg Railsback  
Lexia program – 5/6 split – just 4 students using it – really good program – when student is hung  
up on a concept (i.e. separating syllables) it tells the teacher and then the teacher can sit with  
them and work through what they aren’t understanding  
The program will create worksheets to help teach them – for personal or entire class  
Chromebook use question – is it scheduled? Depends on age/grade – board has a plan for use  
How much time they get used depends on what’s happening in class  
Some teachers try to use them less – kids are losing the component of being able to share  
things, collaborate, tech has taken it away from them – but is’ a necessity  
Talking to the kids about interacting with each other – intramurals are started again and clubs –  
it’s an opportunity to start interacting with each other again  
More Fun/educational clubs will start soon  
Grades/Ages - dependent on what staff member wants to do what – there is no limitation  
How do parents or kids know what clubs are available to them?  
It’s starting slow and will pick up steam as time goes along – there are school announcements in  
the morning – some teachers come to each class at lunch to announce how to sign up
