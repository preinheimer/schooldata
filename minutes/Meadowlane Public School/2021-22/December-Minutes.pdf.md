## Principal’s Report

School year has been going well so far; staff is glad to be back in-class with students.  
Mixed messages on whether it will be in-person learning for the return in January. Board sent out a  
message that all students in the region would be bring home learning devices (chomebook) this week,  
however no official word that we won’t be returning.  
As of now, students will be returning to in-person learning at Meadowlane.  
If there is no return, Phillip or teachers will be reaching out with the steps for the collection of e-learning  
devices, and how/when to come pickup.  
Only change to the use of e-learning devices (if needed), will be that a Device Agreement will need to be  
signed, to pay the \$50 fix fee if it gets damaged.  
For the return in the New Year, the mandatory screening will resume, it is two components:  
• Online screening tool through the board website and;  
• Filling out the screening document, parent initialled, and sent back to the school everyday for  
two weeks  
No real change in staffing. French teacher is on leave for the rest of the school year, currently being  
filled by supply teachers.  
Secreretary Patti Baird filling in for Kim, is looking at other positions and may not return to school in the  
New Year. Kim may not be coming back. Looking to fill position in that outcome.  
Over the break the painting in the school will continue; washrooms will be done.  
WSRB walkthrough happened, and the folding pod walls were looked at as they are no longer a need,  
and they have had things like whiteboards mounted to them. Looking to replace with studded walls that  
will give the classrooms, more outlets, better acoustics, etc.  
Continuing with the finishing of the interior. Ceiling tiles are also on the list, as they are dated.  
Skate Rink boards went up, parent volunteers will be flooding as the weather permits. The rink will not  
be accessible during school hours. Doubtful it will be used by teachers during the school day, too may  
safety measures to contend with. After school hours and weekends, it will be available.  
Lexia presentation was supposed to happen tonight, it has been set to possibly happen at next Council  
meeting.  
First quote came in for the Kindie Area Project. Require a minimum of 3 quotes and board approval  
before moving forward. Prices read out by Mrs. Jones tonight. Project will be happening piece by piece/  
area by area.
