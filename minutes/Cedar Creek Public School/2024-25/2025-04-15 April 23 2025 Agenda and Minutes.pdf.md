Bench (with french quote) as a more permanent option

■ Board has an “Expressions of Sympathy” policy  
on what can and can’t be put in place as a  
memorial - family has already received a letter,  
there will a tree planted by the board in the fall,  
seeds and flowers planted around the tree.  
Board’s position is that when we begin to put  
things like plaques and benches, school spaces  
begin to look like cemeteries, and that can be  
difficult for a learning space, and if down the  
road these things need to be moved (ie:  
construction), or get damaged, it can cause  
more grief over time. Trying to come up with  
other ideas, could we dedicate part of the  
playground? We have a collective need/desire  
to honour Mme Elches, trying to balance how to  
meet needs of the school community and not  
cause harm down the road.  
■ Suggestion of getting/donating french books to  
the library in memory, Mme Elches loved  
reading/providing french reading opportunities  
to her kids.  
■ Is there a way to incorporate french learning  
into the playground?
