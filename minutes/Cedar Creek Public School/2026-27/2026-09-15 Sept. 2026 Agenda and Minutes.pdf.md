## Playground Update

Peter Loker and Alex Ramirez are our new facilities contacts.  
Playground situation has been reassessed and it has been  
determined that the large playground likely has 1-2 years of  
life left. The small playground is unsafe and will be removed  
soon. Our options for the future are:  
\1) Repair the large playground and add new elements.  
This could start in around a month but weather could  
halt progress.  
\2) Wait until spring and increase the scope to replace the  
larger playground as well. The board provides \$15000  
for landscaping, equipment removal, etc, which would  
be provided once.  
Group voted in favour of option 2. We will continue to fundraise this  
year but plan for a spring build. We have approximately \$115000.00  
in the playground fund and another \$16000.00 in the council  
account. Part of the \$16000 can be moved to the playground fund.
