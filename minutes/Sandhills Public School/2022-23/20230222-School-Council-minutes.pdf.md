○ have a BIEP (Board Improvement and Equity Plan) in place which is ministry mandated (similar to a report

○ Our strategic plan has 4 areas of focus:  
■ Achievement  
■ Sovereignty, human rights, and equity  
■ Mental health, wellbeing and engagement  
■ Pathways and transitions  
○ The board has indicators to see if we are meeting our goals in these 4 areas  
○ Some examples of criteria are:  
■ % of students who are reading at grade level by the end of grade 2  
■ % of students who meet or exceed the provincial standard on grade 3 and 6 EQAO  
and on grade 9 EQAO mathematics  
■ % of human rights complaints  
■ % of students receiving modifications  
■ % of students in grades 4-12 who feel school is inclusive and a safe environment  
■ % of students graduating
