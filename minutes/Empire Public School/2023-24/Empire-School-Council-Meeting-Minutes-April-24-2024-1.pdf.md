## Update on Literacy Night

● Literacy night is scheduled for April 30th. It will be a free flow starting from the library and  
in some classrooms. In each class, a teacher will have around 15 minutes talk explaining  
to parents the different tools available for literacy.  

## Quest STEM night Update and Next Steps

● The STEM night was very successful. Immediate feedback from parents and students  
are very positive. Next steps is to report back to QUEST how many students attended  
and wait for their invoice. On the PRO side of things, council has to conduct survey on  
the event and use it survey to write a report to the shool board about the event. The  
survey and the report are part of the conditional requirements to pay the approved grant.  
● Khaled and Lori will check on what is the best way to send the survey to parents.
