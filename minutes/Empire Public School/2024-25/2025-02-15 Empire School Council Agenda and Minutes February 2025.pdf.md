Principal / Vice-principal Report ( Lynda/Lindsay - 10 min)  
● EQAO dates: May 7th - Jun 11th. We will be meeting with teachers to select 2 days during  
that window.  
● We are getting more coat hooks. Things will be moved around a bit to accommodate 30 - 40  
more hooks for the junior kids.  
● Kindie registration is now open, if you know anyone who is planning to register please  
encourage them to register soon as it helps with planning. The board reviews numbers, then  
mid-May we are assigned the number of teachers.  
● The cap for kindies is 29 students - if a lot of students join in Sept reorganizing is necessary.  
Grades 1 - 3 is capped at 20. 4 - 6, there is no cap. 3 / 4 split is capped at 23. This is the  
board average - you can go over 10% over cap.  
● Report cards went home on Mon Feb 10th. There is a new box for Acadience screening -  
this is for language. The assessment is done by a teacher. It takes 1 - 3 mins. The screening  
is done 3 times a year. This is recorded on this term’s report card. It will say “meets  
benchmark” and has a box to check yes or no. Because the initial testing is done so early,  
their current ability is often not reflected on their report card, however, if the benchmark is  
not met, the child’s teacher will have already begun working with them to receive extra  
support to bring them up to benchmark. If benchmark is met mid-year they will not be  
retested at the end of the year. The first kindie screening assesses sounds. Grade 3 and 6,  
ORF testing is used. It is in the experimental phase. We are not yet sure how this will be  
reflected in the report cards.  
● We have started a Food Drive - it is going well. Each day something different is being  
collected. We have a thermometer near the front to measure how much we have collected.
