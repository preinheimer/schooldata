Empire Soccer Goals Project (Khaled - 10 min)  
● This is an investment project. Approval will be contingent to funding. Currently we have no net,  
only one post and soccer is quite popular at Empire. We’d like them to have 2 nets and be able to  
play properly and safely.  
● Our goal posts are currently owned by the city. We have spoken to a vendor but progress was  
halted toward the end of last school year.  
● Different sizes are available, the middle size will work well for both our younger and older  
students. The price range is in the thousand’s ~ \$5000. We do have some funding but will require  
more to cover this.  
● We have reached out to the vendor again for an updated quote - the price remains the same  
however the transportation expense to deliver from Toronto has increased from last year.  
● Prior to purchasing Facilities needs to approve the placement etc. Lynda will follow up to request  
the approval. A requirement is that the vendor is city and board-approved. Also a board-approved  
installer will need to be selected.  
● Brainstorm ideas for fundraising and events (All Council)  
● We discussed new ideas for funding the soccer goals project  
● Flipgive: you can buy gift cards from them and then a percentage goes back to the school.  
● Khaled spoke to Pizzaville and was given a phone number for management to discuss  
sponsorship programs.  
● STEM night and the talent show will both be good opportunities for fundraising and adding  
members to the council.  
● In the past, an envelope to give money rather than participating in fundraising was well-received  
and simple.  
● Donations through WEFI (Waterloo Educational Fund Investment) account. WEFI is the only  
board-approved way to receive cash donations. Fundraising categories can be set up - Empire  
will have an account within the group. There’s a link to it from the School Board website and  
Empire website emp.wrdsb.ca/wefi/  
● Lunch options: Lynda has a PitaPit connection. Ordering is simple, 5% roughly is given back to  
the school. It's usually around \$4, and there is planning to ensure it is accessible for our students.  
Lynda will reach out to her contact, planning potentially for Thursdays starting in Feb. Price range:  
\$4-5.00.
