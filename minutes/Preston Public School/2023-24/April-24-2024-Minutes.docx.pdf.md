## Staffing

\-Our Family of Schools Superintendent, Mrs. Evelyn Giannopoulos, will be moving to the  
Upper Grand DSB as Associate Director. Effective April 29, 2024, Bobbie Chatha will  
become our new Family of Schools’ Superintendent of Student Achievement and Well-  
Being.  
\-2024-25 Tentative Assignments were distributed earlier this month and the first iteration  
of class-building will take place during our May team meeting. Although we cannot make  
decisions based on parent requests, we will consider them when planning for next year,  
with the ultimate goal of making the best placement possible for each of our students  
\-EA and CYW allocations will be out next week. A process of identifying our current  
school year’s “intense needs” occurred earlier this spring and we are always hopeful for  
some additional human resources  
● Academics  
\-Preparations are underway for our spring primary and junior assessments (EQAO). This  
is a three day process with an additional day to catch up in case of absences. Grade 6  
students will participate from May 13-16; grade 3s will be the week after from May 21-24  
\-Gr. 6 transition meetings will be taking place with William G. Davis staff on May 17th  
where both school’s staffs will meet with one another while our students take part in a  
presentation by WGD students. This will take place at Preston. On June 7th, there will  
be a tour of WGD for all of our grade 6 students  
\-Meeting will take place between Preston teachers and WG Davis teachers to make sure  
students individual needs are taken into account and they are placed well in their grade  
7 classes  
● Facilities/Grounds  
\-Facilities Services staff have come in to assess the amount of EWF (Engineered Wood  
Fibre) is required for our Gaga Ball pit. We are anticipating that delivery will occur in the  
next week or so  
\-Our new playground equipment is still on schedule to be installed by Blue Imp on April  
26th. 3 small pieces going in the back field (junior soccer field). Chin up bars, set of  
parallel bars, set of bars that curve down to the ground (reverse push up bar).  
\-Our newly created tile mural has been installed on the hallway leading to the  
gymnasium while the old mural is being donated to the Ken Seiling Waterloo Region  
Museum. Many thanks to Lindsay and Dave Warner for putting the mural together
