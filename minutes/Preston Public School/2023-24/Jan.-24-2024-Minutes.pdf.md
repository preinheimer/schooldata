## Teacher negotiations update

All information is public but summary is that there are  
some fine details that need to be worked out but ETFO has come to an agreement.  
● Academics: Term 2 begins on February 1 with Term 1 reports going home on Feb. 16.  
○ Staff is looking to start up the Strong Start and Home Reading programs again.  
Strong Start will need some trained volunteers and possibly new resources. Staff  
are requesting \$1,000 for new books to update our books.  
● Facilities: Playground equipment update is that purchase orders have been provided to  
Blue Imp by us for the materials and delivery (deposit) and the Board has also submitted  
theirs for the work in the amount of approximately \$7,000. The breakdown will likely be  
approximately \$10,000 from the School Council and \$7,000 from the Board.  
● The Communications Department helped to develop a new panda logo. There is the  
option of creating a web store that can sell spirit wear and sports wear on behalf of the  
school.  
● Staff applied for the Rotary Club of Kitchener Grand River grant proposal to purchase 2  
stationary bicycles: one for junior students and another for primary students. These  
would be used for body breaks have been found to be very effective for some of our  
more energetic students.  
● Kindergarten Registration has begun for students born in 2000 (entering JK this  
September). Corrie Lane has begun making appointments for parents to complete the  
process in person. A Welcome to Kindergarten Information Night has been scheduled for  
Tuesday, May 7 from 6:00-7:00 pm.  
● Marc is working on updating the public calendar on our school website so that all special  
events at the school are posted there.  
● After a four-year hiatus, Public Health will resume the implementation of the ISPA  
(Immunization of School Pupils Act). Reminders have been sent to households in the  
fall. Suspension date would be Wednesday, March 27, 2024. Administrators will receive  
updated lists at the end of each school day.  
● Clubs are continuing. The PHL (Preston Hockey League) just began and students are  
already asking about another soccer club which Marc will be running outdoors once the  
snow melts. On Sunday, the school choir, led by Mr. P-B sang ‘O Canada’ at the KW  
Titans game.  
● Mr P-B and School Council were going to apply for a MusicCounts grant, but School  
Council funded the purchase of new xylophones. Marc shared a video of kids enjoying  
and learning.  
● The staff had a team meeting this afternoon. They are developing groups of teachers to  
create “Dream Teams” that would come together to collaborate on different aspects of  
school life to develop. One main idea is supporting character development.  
● The winner of the Cambridge Fire Fighters Basket Fund was P2. They will be enjoying  
their pizza party next week. The total of items collected was 981 for the entire school.  
Many thanks to Mrs. McIntosh and the Grade 6 leadership team for their work with the  
Fire Department.
