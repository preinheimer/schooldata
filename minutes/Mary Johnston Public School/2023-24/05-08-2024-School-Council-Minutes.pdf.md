## Francine B Gives update on Nutrition for Learning

In terms of funding, \$4000 is the amount the School Council donated. Funding from  
government was \$4157 (this is down around \$2000 from previous year). We are now  
short about \$3000. There have been attempts to fundraise for this. Some of our gap  
will be covered, however, for next year, we may need to think of increasing the  
amount donated by 2-3k. Francine will email the Council and update members on  
what is allotted by the government. She will talk to Cary to close the account and let  
us know exactly what the financial situation is. The costs of NfL items are  
increasing. We will hopefully know by the end of the school year. We may need  
another donation to order what is needed to carry us through to the end of the year.  
We may end up needing to use the funds that were approved for next year to settle  
our account for this year and then increase the donation for the next school year. We  
will need to get approval for this which might be done by remote vote. Going  
forward, it would be better to start voting on the funding for NfL in the February  
meeting. Francine will ensure that the NfL organisation knows that we will cover our  
balance by the end of this year, to make sure they do not need to use any charitable  
donations (such as Tim Horton’s smile cookies) to cover our current shortfall. The  
only amount that cannot be cross funded between schools is what the government  
provides for each school. There is now a plan for this. Our current balance owed is  
\$2900 (\$1200 previously and the current order of \$1700).  
On a related note, Pooja makes a point that Pizza Day is one of our only fundraisers  
that bring in significant funds. Looking forward, we may find that even though we  
have a significant amount of funds in our account right now, it may become  
apparent that we need substantial amounts for the future. There are some things  
such as playground structures and outside learning spaces that may be very costly  
(100k for playground structure).
