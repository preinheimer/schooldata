## Principal/Vice Principal Report

## Grad Committee Update

\-Given the current challenges, we will not be raising funds from the community for Grade 8 grad as we have in the past. There is too much stress on families right now.  
\-A google form will go out to the students to decide what they would like to do, i.e. time capsule, yearbook, other things to go into the yearbook.  
\-Grad photos: our board is still at a “no” for grad photos. The decision is out of the school’s control at the moment - with the board for a decision.  
\-Grad event will probably look a lot like last year with 45 minute blocks during which grads/families walk to the school, receive a swag bag, and get a digital keepsake photo.  
\-We are guessing at around \$15 per student for yearbook. Need to firm this up for the next meeting.  
\-Parent Council will take a vote on whether to help fund the Grad “event” at the April 21 parent council meeting, once the Grad Committee has had a chance to firm up costs. Last year the students got t-shirts, snacks, a few small goodies, re-usable cups, a composite photo, report card and graduation certificate in their swag bag.  
\-We may try to make the photos into digital photo collage, or bitmoji’s.  
\-T-shirts – Michelle will see if the t-shirt company that provides our Spirit Wear could cut us a break on some grad shirts and report back in the April meeting.
