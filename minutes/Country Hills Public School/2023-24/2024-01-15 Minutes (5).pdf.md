## Educator Representative Reports– Mrs. Joanna Kneale

## Primary Update

• Family engagement night enjoyed by the Country Hills community, with a very large  
turnout. Thanks to the leadership of Ms. Nichols & Mrs. McCleman.  
• Kindergarten classes went on a field trip to Huron Natural area on May 17.  
• Jump Rope for Heart event was held on May 30. Students enjoyed skipping, field  
games, sidewalk chalk colouring, prizes and great music. \$663 was raised for the  
Heart & Stroke Foundation.  
• “Welcome to School” event held on June 12 for incoming JK students and new SKs.  
Most families attended.  
• This year’s format was slightly different than in year’s past but the event was  
beneficial to support transitions to school.  

## Junior Update

• Track and Field at Country Hills occurred on May 13th - the weather held off for most  
of the day. Selected students attended the Jacob Hespeler area meet on May 29th;  
another great track and field season!  
• “All About Me” Spirit Day was held on May 30, with many students wearing personal  
items of significance to them! Was great to see the individuality.  
• Last spirit day = June 27 “Wear Red & White” - this is a tradition at Country Hills on  
the last day of school!  
• EQAO ran from June 4-7 for grades 3 & 6 students.  
• Grade 6 students visited Laurentian Public School to support successful transitions to  
grade 7.
