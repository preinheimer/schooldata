● Movember Challenge:  
● Mr. Stuart and other staff members have kicked off MO-vember. This is a month  
for growing mustaches and raising awareness for mental health and cancer  
research. Starting today, until the end of MO-vember, we will be raising money  
and bringing attention to mental health issues and cancer research. There are  
two ways you can help: get 'caught in the act' of performing a random act of  
kindness for someone else or donating \$1 (or more!). Each of the ways will earn  
you a ballot for a draw at the MO-vember assembly, where you could be picked  
to throw a pie in the face of a staff member of your choice!  
● We will be looking for students who are going above and beyond in showing  
kindness…not just simply holding the door for others, although that is kind, but  
also just polite. We will be looking for students who go out of their way to be  
kind…such as making sure someone is ok when they are hurt. Advocating for  
someone who was treated in an unkind way. Including someone in an activity  
that is not normally someone you would hang out with. Checking in with students  
who are quieter to see how their day is going…what can you do to go out of your  
way to be kind?
