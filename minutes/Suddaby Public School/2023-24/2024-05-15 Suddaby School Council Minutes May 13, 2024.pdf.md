## Retirement: Barb (Principal Tomkins)

As I am sure you have already heard, I am retiring at the end of this school year. I have  
had 32.5 years in education and have been lucky enough to enjoy every day of my career.  
Even though my time at Suddaby PS has been short, I feel I have a special connection  
with the Suddaby staff, students, and community. I want to take a moment to express  
my heartfelt gratitude to each of you. Your support, dedication, and collaboration have  
made a significant impact on our school community. Your unwavering commitment to  
our students and their success has been truly inspiring. It has been an honor to work  
alongside such dedicated individuals who are deeply invested in the well-being of our  
students. Thank you for welcoming me with open arms and for your invaluable  
contributions to our school. Though my time here may have been short, the memories  
and connections forged will stay with me forever.

## WRAPSC Report

● Facilities and Procurement: WRDSB has adopted a new process to build playgrounds  
● Under this new process, the money must be raised first. Once the money is in hand,  
Council will go to the Principal, and set up a document for Request for Proposals. Of the  
RFPs received, Council may choose the best (not necessarily the lowest cost) to fit their  
needs.  
● NOTE: there is no list of Board-approved vendors that must be used, except in cases  
where the board has signed an exclusive contract to provide a specific service.  
● Regarding playgrounds, the Board will pay for 1) ground cover, 2) fencing, 3)  
inspections, 4) removal and haulage of old playground  
● The Board will not pay for 1) new equipment, 2) installation of new equipment, 3)  
shipping of new equipment.
