● Suddaby Track and Field:  
○  
In recent years Suddaby’s Track and Field day has been non-competitive. We do  
not record students’ race times, long jump distances, etc. Consequently:  
\1) our students are not given ribbons or any recognition of where they placed  
\2) our students have no way of qualifying for Regionals  
● This approach has the advantage of fostering an equally positive environment for  
all (as no one will be disappointed with their results).  
● This approach also groups children by age, and not gender (unlike regionals), so  
children are not required to choose a gender.  
● Several disadvantages were also raised, including:  
\1) T and F day may be the only opportunity some of our students have to compete, as not  
everyone is equally able to afford extracurricular sports for their children. We are denying those  
children this opportunity.  
\2) A friendly, yet competitive event can engender lessons on sportsmanship  
\3) Our students are at a disadvantage when they go on to other schools and are with  
classmates who have experience participating in full T and F days.  

## Suggestions for a new approach included

\1) Allowing students to participate in either a “competitive” (timed, recorded) or a  
“recreational” (not recorded, just for fun) stream  
\2) Grouping students by age, but recording their results to allow them to go on and  
participate in the gendered Regional competition if the qualify, and wish to do so  
● At this time, a final decision has not been made as to whether to have a  
competitive T and F day at Suddaby this year. Ms. Tompkins welcomes any  
further concerns, suggestions, or comments emailed to her.
