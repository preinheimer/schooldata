## Introduction

What is a School Council?  
● An advisory role committee only; the ultimate responsibility for the administration and  
day-to-day school operations rests with the Principal  
● A means to advise the Principal on any matter pertaining to pupil achievement  
● A voice to provide input to the school Board, when asked for input on policies, plans etc.  

## Review of Council Roles

● Chair - Leads the meetings, co-creates the agenda, and communicates with the school  
community.  
● Secretary - Ensures that they thoroughly and accurately record the meetings  
● Treasurer - Ensures that the funds raised by the Council are tracked separately and  
records are maintained.  
● WRAPSC Rep - Attends 6 WRAPSC meetings, reports back on any items of interest
