## Administrators’ Report

● Progress reports went out today.

one F/T position.  

## Playground update

the WRDSB Facitiltes manager reports that the RFPs for the  
new kindie playground will go out in December, with spring installation planned.  
● The RFP will include all items we had originally requested, and the playground  
committee will have input  
● Although the decision has not been finalized, we are leaning toward using “natural-like”  
materials (i.e. composite) instead of natural (wood) because it is more durable.  
● Movember: During the month of November, students can earn ballots for acts of  
kindness or purchase them for a \$1 donation to the Movember (men’s health-related  
research) campaign. At the end of the month there will be a ballot draw to throw a  
shaving cream pie at a willing staff member.  
● Spirit Days: the next spirit day will be Shining Star Day, November 29th. Wear your  
Suddaby Spirit Wear! December 20th will be Wacky Tacky Day, details to follow.  
● Clubs: The Volleyball, Writing, and Drawing Clubs, and Choirs are up and running,  
Engineering Club will begin within the next 2 weeks.  

## Construction Update

The elevator doors will be installed shortly, allowing use of the  
Lancaster side entrance. The construction end date is planned for September 2025

## Fundraising Report

● One of our Grade 6 teachers has suggested that we increase our popcorn day frequency  
to twice per month, with her students running them. We would need to have an adult  
present to help, and are asking for volunteers. If you have time during the school day to  
come in once per month or even just once, we’d love to hear from you! Please contact us  
at sud-schoolcouncil@wrdsb.ca or reach out through our Facebook page.

before the Shining Star Spirit day.

## Other Business

● ESQ Night: The University of Waterloo Engineering Dept has offered to come to  
Suddaby and run an Engineering Science Quest Night for students and their families.  
Details TBD.  
● Kitchener Ringette: The Kitchener Ringette Association have generously offered to  
come to Suddaby for 2 days to demonstrate/teach the sport. We are looking into the  
feasibility of this; details TBD  
● Strongstart is an early literacy support program run by volunteers in the school.  
Volunteers will be paired with children for 10 30-minute (typically weekly) sessions. Training  
is provided (and mandatory), both online and in person, free of charge.  
● Strong Start Link for training:  
https://strongstart.ca/programs/letters-sounds-and-words/training/  
● The WRDSB now requires all volunteers to complete the volunteer interview form and  
reference check below, and to provide a Vulnerable Sector Check by the WRPS. There  
is a fee for the WRPS check of \$20 if you indicate that it is required for a volunteer  
position. If you wish to volunteer and require assistance covering the WRPS fee, please  
contact Suddaby administration.
