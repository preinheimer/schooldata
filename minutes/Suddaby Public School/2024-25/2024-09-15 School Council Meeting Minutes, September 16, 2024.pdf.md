● Strongstart:  
● Strongstart is an early literacy support program run by volunteers in the school.  
Volunteers will be paired with children for 10 30-minute (typically weekly) sessions.  
Training is provided (and mandatory), both online and in person, free of charge.  
● This year, a new Co-ordinator is also needed. Training is provided free of charge.  
● Volunteers are badly needed; if you would like to help please contact Katherine Akins at  
kakins@rogers.com  
● Strong Start Link for training:  
https://strongstart.ca/programs/letters-sounds-and-words/training/  
● The WRDSB now requires all volunteers to complete the volunteer interview form and  
reference check below, and to provide a Vulnerable Sector Check by the WRPS. There  
is a fee for the WRPS check of \$20 if you indicate that it is required for a volunteer  
position. If you wish to volunteer and require assistance covering the WRPS fee, please  
contact Suddaby administration.

Addendum:  
Due to regulations concerning Council funds (they must be spent on a specific purchase  
rather than a per-child subsidy) and logistical constraints (the way Schoolday accepts  
payments), Council will be paying for the buses required for field trips, rather than using  
the funds to reduce each student’s fee. Each class will get transportation covered for one  
field trip. This change passed via virtual vote on 9/25/2024.
