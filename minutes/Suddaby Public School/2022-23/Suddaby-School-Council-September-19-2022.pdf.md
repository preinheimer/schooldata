## Introduction

What is School Council  
School Councils are an important part of our school communities. The focus of a School Council  
is to improve student learning, achievement, and well-being and enhance the accountability of  
the education system to parents. School Council is:  
● An advisory role committee only; the ultimate responsibility for the administration and  
day-to-day school operations rests with the Principal  
● A means to advise the Principal on any matter pertaining to pupil achievement  
● A voice to provide input to the school Board when asked for input on policies, plans etc.  
● A channel to address issues affecting all, or a significant portion of the school population  
● A group with a shared interest, working together with parents, students, community  
members, staff and administration to build a strong school community.  

## School Council is not

● A forum to address individual issues pertaining to one student or class.

## Admin Update

2022-2023  
We are super happy to have welcomed 467 Stars to Suddaby this year. We have 4 and a half  
sections of kindergarten, 7 English classes and 8 French Immersion classes. Thanks to our  
families who graciously waited until the first day of school to learn class placements.  
A challenge for us in the office is that our secretarial hours were cut by 10 hours per week. This  
impacts how quickly we can respond to families. Thanks for your patience.  
Open House  
It was absolutely wonderful to be able to once again welcome our families into the building and  
we are grateful for the amazing turnout on September 15. The positive energy was incredible.  
Thanks to the members of the school council who sold some of the old Suddaby windows and  
thanks also to the many families who purchased the windows for future projects. Windows are  
still available for families who may have missed out on the opportunity to own a piece of  
Suddaby.  
Transportation Challenges  
Kudos to our Stars who have patiently waited for the bus to arrive for many days. There have  
been several very long waits. STSWR continues to work through the challenges to ensure that  
our students can get transported safely home in a timely manner.  

## Facility Updates

Construction is ongoing and dampers need to be installed. Most of this work is done in the attic

a few adjustments to be made. We hope for an end-of-October completion date. (There is a  
planning meeting for the next construction project this September as well.)
