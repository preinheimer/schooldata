## AdminReport

Science of Reading: Kudos to our staff who are embracing new learning and trying new  
approaches with our Stars. Our K-2 teachers are continuing with Heggerty which teaches  
phonemic awareness and are using sound walls which show students exactly how to shape  
each sound. A new arrival to their teaching toolbox is the UFLI Foundations: An Explicit and  
Systematic Phonics Program. Our Reading Resource Teacher, Mrs. Quint, has begun two  
Empower groups with our grade 2 students and How to Plan Differentiated Reading Instruction  
Resources for Grades K-3 with two groups of grade one students. Our junior teachers are using  
a junior version of this resource. Mrs. Lynch, our Junior SERT, has started Corrective Reading  
with two groups of junior students and she is using Differentiated Literacy Instruction in Grades  
4 and 5 with small groups of junior students. In addition, we have 60 Lexia licenses for students  
who are not in the small groups.  
DreamBox: We have ordered licenses for all of our grades 1-4 (and some grade 5 students)  
and are awaiting the message that we can get those set up and going.  
First Nations, Metis, and Inuit Voices in Elementary Language: Mrs. Lynch,  
Mrs. Tortolo, and Mrs. Taylor are currently enrolled in a three-part learning series. Previously we  
received junior kits with many book sets and this is the PD to support using them in a thoughtful  
and critical manner. A primary book set is on the way.  
Targeted Support Program - Dismantling Anti-Black Racism: Mrs. Stewart, Mrs.  
Lynch, Mme Kraft and Ms. Givlin are attending this 5-part series. As a team, we applied to be a  
part of this initiative last June.

## Events

● When canceling events such as the holiday concert, (former) Mistletoe Market, and  
movie night pizza sales, are we taking away from one group to avoid potentially  
excluding another?  
● Do the benefits of these events outweigh the potential harms?  
● Are these evidence-based decisions? Do we have evidence that these events have  
caused any harm, or would be considered to cause harm by our students or their  
families?  
● Is it realistic to require every event to guarantee equal participation by every student? Is  
a more feasible approach that not everything can be for everyone, but that all students  
must have the chance to participate in some events?
