## Principal’s Report - Rita Givlin

## PRO Grant

Connected with Scott Norton from The Healing of the Seven Generations.  
In an outdoor evening event, he will provide caregivers with a similar experience that he  
will then share with students when he comes the next month to do student workshops.  
The student workshop will focus on grass dancing and drums. The caregiver workshop  
would include this information, as well as focusing on generation traumas that come  
from residential schooling. It would also include extensions for caregivers to use with  
their students, both before and after the student workshops. In our PRO Grant  
application we asked for \$750.  
Workshop Opportunity: We have been working with our Indigenous Learning Team at  
the Board to help us plan for our student workshops with Scott Norton from The Healing  
of the Seven Generations. Scott will share his experience with grass dancing and  
drumming. He will also answer questions that our students develop ahead of time.  
Checked on costing with our Indigenous Learning Team - \$1500  
Mitten Drive: Bethany Church reached out and asked how they could support our Stars  
before the winter holidays. They gave a very generous donation to support the Mitten  
Drive approved by motion at the November school council meeting. Thanks to Wendy  
Saulesleja who purchased 60 pairs of Hot Paws mittens using those funds and sent a  
thank you note to Bethany Church.  
Kindergarten Registration: About 27 new Stars have already registered. We  
appreciate everyone’s help in getting the word out about kindie registration. Please  
encourage families to register ASAP.  
French Immersion Registration: The deadline for kindie families to request FI and be  
in the lottery if needed is the end of January.  
Transportation Change: Letters were mailed to impacted families and the message  
went out to all families on SchoolDay. I reached out to the head of transportation asking  
if there could be another reprieve. His response included the following points:  
\1. 2. 3. 4. I have reviewed the archives related to the closing of Victoria School and the  
grandfathering of transportation. The archives show that transportation was on  
the agenda of the meetings leading to the actual decision however there were no  
mentions of grandfathering transportation in the motion and decision related to  
the closure and redistribution of the students from Victoria School.  
We appreciate that the change is a difficult one for these families. We have  
been communicating for several years about the implementation of the new  
walking zone and have performed several observations of the walking routes.  
The upgrades to the infrastructure, the introduction of the ION and the very low  
volume of traffic do not support the continuation of the transportation services to  
these families.  
The multiple walkabouts have only illustrated that this area does not differ  
significantly from other urban areas and needs to be serviced equitably.  
We need to move forward and enact the removal of transportation which has  
been communicated for many years as we have no grounds to delay further.

StrongStart: Katherine Akins  
StrongStart is planned to begin either the week before or the week after March Break.  
Strongstart is an early literacy support program run by volunteers in the school.  
Volunteers will be paired with children for 10 30-minute (typically weekly) sessions.  
Training is provided (and mandatory), free of charge. Given the pandemic-induced  
disruptions of the last 2 school years, a higher need than usual is predicted. If you can  
help, please see the link below, or contact Katherine Akins at kakins@rogers.com  
https://strongstart.ca/programs/letters-sounds-and-words/training/
