## Pizza Days

Suddaby had our first pizza day last Thursday (May 12th).  
Celebrations - more slices of pizza were ordered than the last pizza day - 435 slices vs.  
353 slices (February 2020). Stars ordered all of the options we had available, including  
Halal pepperoni, a gluten-free crust, and dairy-free cheese. We had Stars donate  
additional money to cover the cost, and we had Stars receive pizza at no cost. Overall,  
the feedback on the taste of the pizza was positive.  
Challenges - The wide variety of options made distribution more difficult. We did have  
some Stars who were disappointed because they thought that they were going to  
receive pizza. This could have been due to confusion with Schoolday, forms, etc. There  
were 4 extra pizzas delivered because of a first-day glitch.  
Next Steps - Sarah will talk to the restaurateur about meeting the drop-off time. Further  
discussion with staff and school council - how can we be more inclusive without taking  
away parent choice? For the 2022-2023 school year, simplifying the menu choices will  
be looked into. We will also include a paper order form for parents who do not wish to  
order using Schoolday.  
EQAO: Grade 6 EQAO will be written from June 6th to 9th, with June 10th as a

## Communication of Council Roles to Parents

Many of our Members have  
been on the Council for several years now, and it was suggested that we clarify  
for new members and parents who may wish to join us what the Council does,  
what subcommittees and roles exist within the Council, and what options exist for  
people who may wish to help out in some capacity but don’t know if there is a  
way to do so. We agreed this was a good idea, and have begun putting together  
a synopsis.
