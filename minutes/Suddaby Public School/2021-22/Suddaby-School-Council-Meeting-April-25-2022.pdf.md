FI Review Consultation Meetings  
Suddaby was very well represented at the admin/staff consultation meeting and our participating  
staff members asked great questions! Information has gone out on School Day for parent  
sessions and we encourage all interested families to participate.  
The proposal is that effective September 2023, FI would begin in grade 2. All grade one  
students would have an hour each day of French instruction/learning in French. The amount of  
French in grades two to six would increase from 50% to 58% to make sure the required minutes  
of French instruction are met.  
School Council suggested timetabling students together when appropriate for increased student  
well-being. Teacher contacts were discussed. Opportunities for students include clubs and  
outdoor times at nutrition breaks where students are able to be together. There were also  
questions about French teacher staffing.  
Grade 6 Celebration - June 23, 2022, in the afternoon  
The grade 6 team is planning a grade 6 celebration to mark our grade 6 Stars last year at  
Suddaby. It is a big group graduating this year. As we move to be more mindfully inclusive and  
celebrate each one of our grads, we are no longer offering awards or printing certificates for  
students. What remains is a focus on celebrating and honouring our Stars with the popular  
slideshow including baby pictures, speeches from admin and classroom teachers, a display of  
student art, and student performances followed by cupcakes and punch. We will likely hold our  
celebration across the street at Bethany Church to avoid complications due to the construction.  
The usual Suddaby Dress Policy will apply and regular school clothes are welcome.  

## Construction Update

May 2, 2022, the summer construction project begins. Air conditioning is being installed on the  
top floor and new heritage windows are being installed in the front. A section of the tarmac will  
be fenced off. Initially, we are losing the use of the special education offices and room 24 and  
the staff room. After a month, the staffroom and room 24 should be available again and we will  
lose the use of the library for the month of June. This was the least disruptive plan for students.  
The hope is to be completed before the return to school in September. Staff will not be allowed  
in the building over the summer.

## EQAO Update

EQAO is online this year. There are 4 sessions in language and 4 sessions in math, for both the  
Grade 3 and Grade 6 assessments. Educators who are involved with the assessment have  
looked at the Framework, which covers suggested timing and the material that will be covered.  
They have also done Board PD at the last staff meeting. There is a sample test, which provides  
access to the online tools, and sample questions from both the language and math sections.  
Sample answers from both the language and math sections are also available to educators. As  
an EQAO team, we will be meeting next week to further discuss logistics and finalize dates.  
Volunteers in the School and Police Vulnerable Sector Checks  
This is covered in procedure 1415, which is entitled the Use of Volunteers. This procedure  
references administrators ensuring that volunteers are screened and that a reference is verified.  
It includes the forms for these tasks. It also references volunteers completing a police  
vulnerable sector check “if supervising students who are not in direct proximity of a staff  
member or if deemed necessary by the principal.  
” The fee is \$20 for volunteers to receive this  
check and we’ve created a letter that volunteers can show when paying. If this creates a barrier  
for prospective volunteers, please reach out to Rita or Sarah.  
School Council discussed the barriers that exist (cost, location to pick up checks, the timing of  
pickups.) Questions were raised about whether this was equitable. Rita will forward these  
concerns to our Superintendent.  
Rain Bell Protocol  
We have 3 educators supervising outside in the mornings, as a part of our supervision schedule.  
We have allocated all of our supervision time, including SMACA. If we move students inside  
when it is raining in the morning, we would have 1 educator per floor and no supervision  
outside. This would also create cohorts of a significant number. The application of the current  
COVID protocols in place including sanitizing before entering the building would be less  
stringent. Hopefully, we will be able to revert to past practice in the fall.  
School Council shared that spending time in the rain in the mornings is quite difficult. Andrea  
shared how the rain bell protocol worked before the pandemic and the resulting protocols  
(sanitizing and consistent door entry).  

## Pizza Day

We are going to try doing a pizza day each week for 6 weeks. This will start on May 12th and  
end on June 16th. We are going to use AAA Pizza and Wings. Their costing is competitive and  
they have options that our Stars may require, such as gluten-free crust, Halal cheese and  
pepperoni, and vegan and lactose-free cheeses.  
We will take orders on School-Day and will have an option for caregivers to donate pizza slices  
for others. We will also have an option for caregivers to request pizza for their student(s) if they  
are unable to pay.  
School Council asked about nutrition facts and about whether this can be peanut free. Sarah  
will call AAA Pizza and Wings to find out.

## Fundraising

We are trying to increase student voice at Suddaby. Ideally, our fundraisers will focus on  
charities that impact our Suddaby Stars or ones that they become aware of during their learning.  
For example, we have a group of grade 6 students who want to fundraise to plant a tree as their  
parting gift. This idea was generated by the students and efforts will be led by them. Earlier this  
year, one class raised money for The Healing of the Seven Generations.  
Council had asked about doing a dance-a-thon for Ray of Hope which is a wonderful local  
charity doing great work in our community. However, this involves our students in meeting adult  
goals.  
Could we support Ray of Hope as a group of Suddaby adults instead?  
School Council asked about a fundraising club. Another suggestion was student reps, which  
they thought may allow more communication between the School Council and the different  
classes.
