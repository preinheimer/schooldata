## Principal’s Report – Mary Sue

• Winter Walk to School Day  
o On February 1st it was Winter Walk to School day. There were 5 educators outside, and  
we saw lots of participation (360 students walked to school out of a total of 430  
students!). In addition there were fewer drop offs in front office (e.g. from students  
arriving late).  
o The initiative came from student transportation services to cut down on traffic  
congestion and the idling of cars; car traffic in the morning has been somewhat  
dangerous at times.  
o We have received feedback that parents and students would like to have other seasons  
for walk to school days.  
￼  

## Report Cards

o Term 2 is just starting and report cards will be coming home on Feb 15.  
o Report cards are not a quick process; the principal reads all report cards and then  
returna them to teachers for corrections and revisions. It is a very busy time.  
• IEPs  
o With term 2 starting, IEPs may be revised.  
o If your child has an IEP, you should receive a Term 2 IEP for your child by the end of  
February.  
• Kindergarten Registration  
o Kindergarten registration is happening right now.  
o While it seems very early, it helps us because staffing process happens in April.  
o Starting in April we have to think about the next year (the number of classes, etc.), and  
the more we know in advance the better.  
o If you have neighbours with young children, please let them know that registration is  
open. To register, families can go to the WRDSB board website.  
https://www.wrdsb.ca/kindergarten/  
o Families first register online, then will get an email from Westvale’s secretary, and will  
set up a time to come in to fill out the paperwork and receive a couple of goodies for  
the student.  
o A squirrel called Chip is the logo for kindergarten across the board; you get a few things  
when you register (like bookmark) and items have Chip on them.  
o There will be an evening in June for parents with children starting in JK – date will be  
released when finalized.  

## Find Freezie Event

o We did an in-school event with a stuffed snowman called Freezie.  
o It was hidden around the school for the week, and students were very excited to find it.  
o Students had to photograph it to prove they had found it.  
o There was a winner in each category (class 3-4A, room 14, class K4, class K2, class 5B  
and class 4A). ACE class and ISP classes also both engaged really well with the event.  
• Fleece Tie Blankets for the Humane Society  
o Our Library clerk, Eva Ryczko would like to do a fleece tie blanket project with the  
students.  
o The Humane Society came last year, and students learned about animals in need in their  
shelter.  
o She would like students from grades 4-6 to be involved. They would meet once a week  
in the library during nutrition break.  
o Eva would like to buy polar fleece and has instructions to create the blankets from the  
humane society website.  
o She would like to make 10 blankets, which requires 14 yards of fleece at \$8.99 per yard.  
This would be \$125 (\$142.22 with tax).

voted in favour, and the motion passed.  
• Anonymous Bullying Reporting Tool  
o There is a new web-based bullying reporting tool that can be used by students.  
o The student can either include their information or list themselves as anonymous.  
o If a student reports bullying, the principal gets an email and deals with the bullying.  
o If the reported bullying is severe, the principal will try to identify the person who is the  
victim to offer support.  
o This tool is aimed at grade 7-12 and is a result of the student survey for student  
engagement as some students are not reporting bullying.  
o Bullying can also be reported at any time directly to the principal by either parents or by  
students.

• Westvale Green Team – (on behalf of Andrea Brunsting and Helen Quinton)  
o In January the Westvale Green Team began meeting. We have about 15 students  
committed to helping Westvale develop initiatives to be a greener community.  
o The team decided that the most important initiative to begin with is to educate students  
about the many small things that they should be mindful of in the classroom to create  
less waste and safe energy.

classes that share their ideas for a better eco-friendly Westvale classroom which they  
will be sharing in the coming weeks.  
o They will be putting information together about how lunch room plastics should be  
disposed of at home to make sure they are recycled when they are sent home  
(boomerang lunches).  
o There was a question from a parent about yogurt pots being recycled at the school (as  
they are very messy in lunch bags when they are sent home) and Mary Sue explained  
that there is an issue with how much plastic can be recycled from the schools. They set a  
maximum amount of recycling the school can leave out, and unfortunately that means  
the school cannot have students leaving their recyclables from lunches behind. She  
suggested that reusable containers may be a better solution.

## WRAPSC Update – Richard

• You can find the meeting minutes from the WRAPSC meeting here: https://www.wrdsb.ca/our-  
schools/get-involved/wrapsc/minutes/  
• In the January meeting we heard from healing of the 7 generations (program assist in education  
and assistance of first nation communities).  
• The WRDSB student trustees also spoke. These positions are elected positions and are intended  
to give students a voice in the board.  
o Last year they conducted a Thinking out Loud survey for student perspectives. Concerns  
were raised that most of this type of input is for grades 7 and higher (student trustees  
and voting, grade 7 or higher). Questions were raised about student voices for the  
younger grades, and suggestions were that there could be parent reps or student reps  
for those younger grades.  
• Also discussed how to connect for round tables and discussions and how to make meetings  
more interesting. Surveys to WRAPSC reps each week which help determine the topics for the  
next month’s session.  
• The suggestion from WRAPSC to encourage attendance and involvement in council meetings is  
to make your meeting 50% boring (required discussions and communications, fundraising, etc.)  
and 50% interesting (speakers, nutritionists, online safety – police has a speaker on this, fitness  
counsellors, psychologists, etc.). It was noted that schools could use the line item from the  
school budget to pay for a speaker for council meetings.  
￼  
• The suggestion was also to use email to get the word out about meetings and to review rules for  
council meetings at the beginning of the year.  
• WRAPSC and PIC talking about amalgamating, but WRAPSC does not want this.

## Other Business

• Spirit Wear  
o Pegasus does spirit wear; they are a local company  
o We would do T-shirts and hoodies only, and they would build the online store for us to  
use. Parents order and pay online, the orders ship to the school and each item is  
labelled with the student’s name or parent’s name.  
o With this type or ordering, there would be no school day fees.  
o This year, we will run a Limited Edition Westvale PS design. It will be white text on navy  
(like a dark denim colour). Next year, the new logo will be available and spirit wear will  
be offered in the fall of 2023 with the new logo.  
o The quality seemed quite good in the samples. Items would be from the Gilden or ATC  
brand. ATC seemed nicer, but the exact type would be what’s available at the time of  
ordering.  
o The cost of the T-shirts is \$14 (including tax), and hoodies are \$40, including HST. This is  
the COST, to make this a fundraiser we would have to add \$2 to t-shirt and \$2 to the  
hoodie making them available for \$16 and \$42.  
o There is also an option to add a custom name (e.g. on the sleeve), which would be an  
optional upcharge of \$8.  
o The online store would be open for 2 weeks. All orders would need to be submitted  
within that time frame, then we would have a 2-3 week turnaround from store closing  
date until the items were ready for distribution.  
o Bobbi-Joe wants to run it 2 weeks before March break, and after March Break suggests  
we could do a spirit day (blue shirt or Westvale shirt day).  
o Mary Sue would send a school day blast to let parents know it’s coming.  
o There was a suggestion to make sure we are transparent in the communication  
regarding how much profit we are making per shirt, as well as the fact that the logo is a  
LIMITED EDITION and the new logo will be available in Fall 2023.  
o Bobbi Joe motioned to run the fundraiser, Lynda seconded; all voted in favour, motion  
passed.  
o Bobbi Joe to work on a draft for the school day communication.
