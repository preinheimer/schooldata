## Sovereignty/human rights/equity

• Making sure there are fewer suspensions to increase time in class and  
looking for other ways to help the students (e.g. with mental health or  
developmental supports); only for grades 4-8, we do not suspend  
grades 1-3  
• Mental health/well being/engagement (for students, staff, and caregivers)  
￼  
• Pathways and transitions (grade 6 to grade 7; from grade 8 to 9; from high  
school to whatever pathway you choose e.g. apprenticeship).  
• We know if a student has 16 credits by end of grade 10, they have a  
higher chance of graduating  
• We want to support different pathways, not just university and college  
• De-streaming is being done to make sure that students from different  
backgrounds have the same opportunities  
• French Immersion (FI) and Core French Changes  
o There are several changes coming to the FI and core French programs  
o Details are available here: https://www.wrdsb.ca/french/  
o Summary  
• Grade 1 FI will not available in the 23/24 year  
• FI will be available from grade 2-12  
• The purpose of FI is NOT to create fluency in French, rather it is to have students  
have confidence, proficiency, and achievement in all French as a Second  
Language programs  
• It is being delayed, in part, to give equity of access to all students (sometimes  
parents aren’t aware they need to apply in kindergarten)  
• Staffing challenges are also related to why we are transitioning; it has been hard  
to get quality French teachers  
• In grade 1, if you were in core (that is not in French Immersion), you would have  
had 5 x 30 min periods of French  
• Starting in grade 1, students will have 1 hour per day (10 periods per week) in  
French (this is 10x30 minutes, or approximately double what is currently offered  
to the core students  
• To accommodate the additional French instruction time, art will be in French in  
grade 1 (this includes music, drama, dance, visual arts)  
• This increase allows students starting in grade 1 to have more exposure to  
French overall  
• Starting in grade 2, for French Immersion students, 58% of their day will be in  
French. Currently it’s 50% in French.  
• French Immersion Students going forward will have  
o Taught in English: English language, math, science  
o Taught in French: Phys Ed, Health, Art, Drama, Music  
• The hope is that students will be more successful in math when instruction is in  
English  
• Math and Science is NOT TAUGHT IN FRENCH starting in grade 7 so this matches  
better with the transition, it will also allow FI students to perform better on  
standardized testing, which is delivered in English  
• Integrated Support Plan  
o The integrated support plan was cancelled by the board; parents who are affected have  
been notified  
o This includes 6 students who are currently in a congregated classroom. These students  
have Anxiety along with a learning disability or ASD.  
o The affected parents asked them to reconsider  
o Trustees directed the board to do more research about why the program was being  
cancelled, and report back.  
o This report was supposed to be delivered on Monday, but the board meeting was  
cancelled due to weather, so the results are pending.
