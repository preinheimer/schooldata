## Principal’s Report – Mary Sue

• A Primary literacy night will be happening on May 2nd  
o Primary English and FI teachers will be presenting; starting in the library and move to  
workshops in the classrooms  
o We will also cover the changes to the home-reading program (which has been  
discontinued) and the importance of phonics and the decodable texts the students are  
using  
o Please register online if planning to attend  
• June 10th we will host a Welcome to Kindergarten evening.  
o Different classrooms will be open and families can rotate through the classrooms to  
experience the learning stations in the classrooms.  
o Parent volunteers welcome for this event.  
o June 10 from 6-7 pm  
• This year EQAO will be happening for Grade 6 students from May 27-30, and for Grade 3  
students from June 4-7  
o The test is entirely online  
o There is a voice to text features available as part of the program  
o The test assesses writing, reading, and math  
• EQAO results from last year (2022/2023) were reviewed  
￼  
o The goal is to have students testing at a Level 3 (which is the provincial standard). Level  
4 means students are exceeding expectations of the grade level, and anything below  
level 3 means students are not meeting the grade-level expectations.  
o Grade 3 Results  
▪ We saw improvements from the prior year across reading, writing, and math.  
▪ There is some concern about the number of level 1 students we have in Math  
and Writing; these are areas of concern and development  
o Grade 6 Results  
▪ Reading and writing are close to board and provincial standards, but compared  
to province and board level, there were fewer level 4s at Westvale for writing.  
▪ We have seen improvements from 2021/2022 school year vs 2022/2023 school  
year for math, however there are still 40% of kids that are at a level 2  
▪ The number of students at Level 2 for math is also a concern at the board and  
provincial level as the proportion of kids at level 2 is about the same  
o For FI students, math was instructed this year in English (where as prior years it was  
instructed in French). Because students were learning math in French and were tested  
in English, we may see some differences this year related to teaching students math in  
English and then testing them in English  
• Mary Sue looked into getting temporary tattoos with our logo on it. These must be sourced  
through a board-approved vendor. The one vendor she reached out to quoted 50 cents each  
tattoo, but had a 3,000 minimum, which puts the cost of this item out of reach. Mary Sue will  
reach out to the next vendor on her list to find out if they have better pricing available.

## Book Fair Update – Tonya and Steph

• The book fair was a huge success once again. Thanks to all for donating your books, and thanks  
to all the volunteers (but especially to Liz) who made this happen. It’s a tremendous amount of  
work, but such an engaging and wonderful event. Thanks Liz!!!  
• Every kid got a book and it raised a lot of excitement over books and reading  
• We had donations above and beyond the sales of the book in some cases (e.g. paying for \$2 in

generosity!

beneficial as it once was. Mary Sue also points out that a lot of their product offerings are toys  
and not books.  
• Of the proceeds (\$1314), 50% will be donated to Nutrition for Learning. We have had a high  
need for food from Nutrition for Learning this year and would like to give back.
