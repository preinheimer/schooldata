## Parents Reaching Out (PRO) Grants - Tonya

• The PRO Grant comes out every year and the goal is to connect and support parents within the  
school community. https://www.wrdsb.ca/pic/parents-reaching-out-pro-grants/  
• This year we have automatically received \$500 with no application! (Usually we have to fill out a  
form, but not this year.)  
• There are, however, some limits on what we can spend this on. If it is not spent, it will be  
reclaimed at the end of the year.  
• We have to meet one of the following criteria:  
o Fosters parent engagement  
o Addresses social inclusion and racism  
o Provides translation of posters or literature  
o Demonstrates respect for parents as valued partners in their children’s education (e.g.  
purchasing parenting books for loan)  
o Informs parents about the Ontario curriculum and expectations (e.g. Literacy Night)  
• We usually work with the principal to determine what’s needed in the school, and we vote on  
how to use the money.  
• This year, it could be about math or literacy (especially looking at some of our EQAO results  
from last year).  
• There was feedback that the Literacy night was really helpful last year, and it might have been  
even better earlier in the year. The literacy night was organized and run by several teachers to  
educate parents on how the new Science of Reading approach to literacy is helping students to  
learn to read and gain fluency.  
o Information can be found here (https://www.wrdsb.ca/learning/programs/structured-  
literacy-multi-year-plan-slmyp/). We are phasing this in and are currently in year 4.
