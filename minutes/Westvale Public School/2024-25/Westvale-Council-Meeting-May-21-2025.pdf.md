## WRAPSC – Jen Benninger

• Jen did not attend the meeting, so she is relaying the meeting minutes that were sent.  
• They had a speaker in to talk about long term and capital planning (board planner came to  
speak). They looked at aging infrastructure and accessibility. The board submit requests to the  
province, and any shortfalls in funding are covered by the board. Sometimes the province might  
withhold funding because some projects are stalled due to lack of funds.  
• Schools in our region are fully utilized; Toronto has many underutilized, so we are efficient.  
• A portapac is only built if you permanently need 6+ classrooms (ours only has 4 classrooms, so it  
wouldn’t be built today).  
• They are removing sibling priority from FI lottery (which means everyone has an equal chance of  
getting into French Immersion regardless of whether an older sibling is already enrolled in FI).  
• French Immersion applications are due Jan 2026. Students can no longer apply to Catholic and  
Public Boards.  
• Once a student is enrolled in FI, you have to go through an involved process including  
superintendent approval to move FI kids back to the English stream; this has been true for the  
last 2 years, but is not well known or communicated.  
• Our school board is not required to offer FI; we are an English Language school board. We still  
have a shortage of French teachers.  
• If you know a child that needs an elevator, it takes time to build, and parents are encouraged to  
let the schools know as soon as possible so they can make a plan.  
• WRAPSC and PIC magnets being handed out at grade 9 info nights.  
• The Director of Education is going forward with many recommendations with PIC.  
• They need volunteers for PIC going forward; it’s a 2 year commitment.  
• The next WRAPSC meeting is October 7th; looking for a Westvale volunteer for next year.
