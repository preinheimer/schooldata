## SCIS Speaker – Chandelle

• Every school needs a SCIS (Safe Caring and Inclusive Schools) committee.  
• The goal is to make sure kids feel safe and included.  
• Westvale already has one, which has been in place for several years.  
• Examples of SCIS initiatives are things like climate captains, equity wall to showcase student  
creativity, annual showcase for student belonging and growth, community partnerships, etc.  
• There is a focus on student incidents, as these have been rising, and we want to reduce that. In  
this context a “student incident” would be things like situations where a teacher needs first aid  
because of something a student did, where the class needed to be taken out of the room due to  
the student’s actions, where student is disruptive and causing issues, or for student to student  
or student to teacher violence. These types of incidents are the most prevalent from JK to Grade  
\2.  
• SCIS is focused on how to intervene and help parents and how to help stop this from happening.  
• Kids want to be more inclusive, but some are having problems with emotional regulation.  
• We want to make sure kids feel safe to talk to an adult.  
￼  
• We are also always looking always for parents to tell PIC (the Parent Involvement Committee)  
what they want and need. Parents are encouraged to approach PIC to express needs and  
experiences.  
• If anyone would like to reach out to Chandelle directly, she can be reached at  
chandellemmonette@gmail.com  
• PIC is generating QR codes on magnets, with the goal that each student will take one home and  
can scan the code and is then directed to express any concern they might have.  
• The PIC Website is available from the main WRDSB website, but it’s still under construction. It  
will have more information soon about SCIS.
