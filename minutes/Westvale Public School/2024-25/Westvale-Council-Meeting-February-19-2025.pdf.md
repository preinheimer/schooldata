## WRAPSC – Jen

• The last meeting they talked about cell phone policies, and was more geared toward secondary  
students.  
• They spoke about how its being implemented and what they’ve seen.  
• They are having a hard time with apple watches, because they aren’t specifically mentioned in  
the policy.  
• If students are given the option to use their phones as a reward, they choose to use them  
instead of engaging in face-to-face interactions with peers.  
• There are reports of younger children bringing phones to school, which is, of course, not  
allowed.  
• PIC was there, and were looking to strengthen parent engagement.  
• For the PRO Grant, they received 69 applications and all but one were approved. 55% of schools  
applied.  
• PIC is advocating for a longer window for applications to the PRO grant next year. The  
recommendation is to sent by October for submission in January.  
• There was a large group discussion around AI.  
• They also spoke about approved vendors for certain types of items, such as board games.  
Michelle can help if anyone needs to access these lists of approved vendors.
