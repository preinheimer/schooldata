Julie to send out WEFI information on School Day explaining how WEFI

Peter Cheung is looking into “Recycling for Education”  
. . .  
“Recycle for “Education is an innovative fundraising program that helps schools to raise money  
through the power of recycling .  
We are excited to offer your school the opportunity to join an eco-friendly project that not only  
benefits the environment, but also helps you generate additional funds for the school.  
Through this recycling event, we will pay \$3.45 for each large bag of clothing and other textiles  
that is collected. Research has indicated that events like these typically collect between 250 -  
300 bags, providing an estimated \$862-\$1,035.  
In addition to clothing, we can also monetize books, e-waste( electronics working or not), and  
housewares (cutlery, glassware, pots and pans, and so on / Toys / Games / Ornaments and any type of knick-knack /  
housewares /Sporting goods).  
This money can be used for a variety of educational programs and activities at your school.  
Let's join forces and create a meaningful impact on our community.  
We hope you join us in supporting this cause!  
The OFCP raises funds by recycling unwanted clothing, household items and unwanted  
(working or not) electronic items. We do so to raise money for people with cerebral palsy  
across Ontario. At this moment, we support more than 4,000 people with cerebral palsy  
thanks to the support of organizations like yours, and we are in need of your help.
