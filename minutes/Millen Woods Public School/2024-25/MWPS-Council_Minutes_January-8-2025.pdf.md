are enjoying it.

day. We should consider using ESQ for one of our events next year to thank them for their  
continued support.

## Dance-a-Thon Student Survey: Karen

\- Students were surveyed about whether they wanted to keep the dance a thon or change to a new  
event. The kids overwhelmingly want the dance a thon back. The kids were invited to share other  
ideas, but most of the ideas shared are already in place (winter play day, class reward days, etc.)  
so we will move ahead with the dance a thon in the same format as last year, with the second side  
of the gym open for alternative activities (cornhole, etc.)  
\- Rebecca suggested having glow sticks for purchase. Karen raised concerns about student equity  
since there are already photo booth strips for purchase. Suggested that we set a school  
fundraising goal, use a thermometer chart, and if the school raises the total funds then council  
will purchase enough glow sticks for all the kids  
\- Messaging to indicate that the funds are being raised towards a gaga ball pit for 2025-2026  
school year installation (expected cost was around \$3k last year, we have allocated \$1,500 of this  
year’s budget towards the gaga ball pit with the remainder of the funds to come from next year’s  
budget)  
Action item: Ashley to reach out to the DJ to get possible dates for March, and then coordinate with  
Karen for gym availability. Following the meeting, March 5th has been booked with the DJ for the  
Dance-a-thon! More correspondence to follow, and volunteers will be needed.
