## Admin Report: Karen

\-  
Volleyball intramurals have started. Grade 6 travel team has been formed – no cuts were made, and  
everyone gets the opportunity to play a portion of the games  

\-  
Mme Kazemzadeh will be taking a temporary position with ETFO starting in December until the end  
of June. The position is a  
“  
member mobilizer  
”  
and involves advocacy for students and public  
education. Mme K will stay on until her position can be filled with an LTO.  

\-  
CBC Radio Contest submission was recorded today – the whole school came together to perform  
one indigenous song.  

\-  
Mme Marschuetz is retiring at the winter break. A permanent experienced French Immersion teacher  
has been hired to start in January.  

\-  
Safe Caring and Inclusive Schools committee is up and running. The mandate is to raise awareness  
for the various days of recognition through posters & announcements and facilitate events  
throughout the year around Indigenous learning.  

\-  
Ministry Screening happened in K – Grade 2 classes. The screening assesses the reading program.  
Results will be shared with parents in the next few weeks. The screening happens 2-3 times per  
year and it is common for students to not pass the first screening as it happens early in the year and  
the teachers haven  
’t had a chance to teach the specific skills at the time of the screening. Teachers  
will be providing contextual information to parents to help them interpret the results. The screening  
will be coming for grades 3-6 in the future but the teachers are working to understand the program  
now so that they can be prepared to help students succeed

Math and Literacy: Karen  

\-  
Karen gave a presentation on the Board’  
s initiatives towards literacy, including the BIEP goals, MAAP  
math plan, and SLMYP literacy plan. These plans cover goals to support students in many areas  
including math, literacy, sovereignty human rights and equity, mental health well-being and  
engagement  

\-  
The Board website has information on each of the plans and links will be shared  

\-  
The BIEP is a board-wide plan. Millen students are already meeting the targets established in the  
BIEP based on our EQAO results.  

\-  
Teachers have been undergoing a lot of learning to implement these plans. Millen is in year 2 of a  
multi-year plan.  

\-  
Karen gave a demonstration of Heggerty literacy teaching. Council performed very well 😊  

\-  
Math presentation to be given by Karen next month
