## NORTH STAR

To ensure that identity and social location no longer predict outcomes, TOGETHER we will transform  
our systems, structures, physical environments and practices to address and embrace the lived  
experiences, abilities, needs and stories of students and staff to ensure success for ALL.  

## WRDSB VISION FOR MATH

By providing all students access to high-level, equitable, and inclusive mathematics instructional  
practices, we will ensure that identity and social location no longer predict outcomes.  
School, system and classroom leadership, and learning, is foundational to creating the conditions in  
which students will thrive in their mathematics education.  
These goals span all grade levels, from kindergarten to graduation, and in this way  
the WRDSB Math Action Plan is for ALL WRDSB EDUCATORS.
