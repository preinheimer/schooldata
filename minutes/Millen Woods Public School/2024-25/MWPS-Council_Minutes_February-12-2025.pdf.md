Winter Wonderland Feedback:  
\- Huge thank you to the subcommittee and to the school community for running such a wonderful  
\- Feedback on this year's event:  
• Mix up with the date of the event, caused some of the original plans to be rejigged at the  
last minute  
• Entertainment was geared more towards the younger children, next year maybe we can go  
to the original plan geared towards older children with the Star Performance performers  
• Kids LOVED the mural - look at getting a larger mural next year  
• Families asked if they could order pizza by the slice, next year look at ordering a few extra  
pizzas and selling slices  
• Audio system wasn't loud enough - for next year, the entertainment needs to be able to  
bring a mic that connects to a Bluetooth speaker or they need to be stationary  
• Tea station was very popular, but should have an adult volunteer watching it as kids were  
getting into it  
Hot chocolate had several spills - encourage reusable cups with a lid for next year  
• Hot chocolate quantities - this year we ordered 200 and it was too much, the prior year we  
ordered 150 and it was not enough. The vendor threw in some extra this year which may  
have contributed to us having too much.  
• Winter wonderland was on a Tuesday - because the kids had pizza for lunch during the day,  
it was a lot of pizza to have it for dinner orders too  
School Photos: Dan  
Edge Imaging and Lifetouch will both offer 10% of proceeds back to the school as well as various  
perks to the school and staff  
Edge is 100% Canadian-owned, and Lifetouch is US owned  
Litetouch pricing is lower than Edge  
There seems to be no reason to lock into a multi-year contract  
Edge has a dedicated sales rep for our school and Lifetouch is through a call centre. Mrs Moore is  
happy with both.  
Do we prioritize supporting Canadian businesses even though prices to families are slightly higher?  
We like the Canadian aspect now but are concerned about prices - 30% higher  
Edge is our current vendor and will do the class photos this spring.  
Action item: Karen to go back to the reps with these questions: Lifetouch - do they do digital-only packages,  
and what do they charge for that? Edge - can they price match the Lifetouch packages? For both - can we  
do class composites at the beginning of the year instead of in the spring?  
Action item: Karen will send out the answers to the questions once received and then Ashley will do an  
e-vote.
