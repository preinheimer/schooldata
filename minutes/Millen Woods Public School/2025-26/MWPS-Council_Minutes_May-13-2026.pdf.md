MW Public School Council Fundraising Report 2025 - 2026  
The purchase of the new Kindergarten playground equipment has been made!!! This is a huge milestone that we could not have achieved had this  
community not rallied and raised an astounding \$50,000! The WRDSB has advised that the installation will be in August, with the grand opening in  
September 2025!  
Thank you to our wonderful Millen Woods school community, without your support, we would not have been able to provide the much-loved  
enrichment opportunities at our school, which included new equipment for the nutrition break bins, playground maintenance, new classroom  
projectors, decodable texts for students, and busing for class trips and area track meet. We also hosted these FREE family events: Meet the  
Community Night, Winter Wonderland, Movie Night, Garden Night and Lolly-palooza!  
Each year, Council will provide a report illustrating the total earned through our fundraising campaigns and how the money was spent throughout the  
year. Please refer to the tables below for the breakdown of funds for the 2025-2026 school year.  
The major fundraising focus for the 2026-2027 school year will be to cover the budgeted costs for enrichment opportunities within the school (i.e.  
guest speakers), school field trip busing costs, track and field busing costs, and playground enhancements (balls, scoops, etc.).  
At the end of this report, we have included the anticipated Fundraising Calendar for the 2026-2027 School Year. Families are never obligated to  
participate in the fundraisers; however, it is greatly appreciated. For those wishing not to participate in sale-type fundraisers, families are always  
able to provide directed donations to Millen Woods through WEFI online at: https://www.wrdsb.ca/wefi/donate/ (under "Fund" drop-down, ensure you  
select "Millen Woods - 0519"). Donations of over \$15 to WEFI will receive a tax receipt.  
Planning for the 2026 - 2027 School Year  
Our first Council meeting for the 2026-2027 school year will be held on Wednesday, September 23rd, 2026, at 6:30 pm in the School Library!  
The School Council for the 2026-2027 school year will be formed at our first meeting on September 23, 2026. This important meeting marks the  
beginning of a new year of collaboration between caregivers, staff, and school leadership as we work together to support student success and a  
vibrant school community.  
School Council is a valuable forum for sharing ideas, providing feedback, and helping shape the student experience through fundraising, events,  
and school improvement initiatives. We encourage all interested caregivers to consider getting involved!  
Positions available each year include:  
• Chair or Co-Chairs - Lead the Council and serve as a liaison between the Council, school administration, and caregiver community.  
Page 1 of 6  
• Secretary - Records and shares meeting minutes and supports Council communication.  
• Treasurer - Manages Council funds and provides regular financial updates.  
• Caregiver Representatives - Voting Members - Elected caregivers who participate in decision-making and attend meetings regularly.  
• Caregiver Representatives - Non-Voting Members - Caregivers who participate in discussions and support activities but do not vote on  
Council decisions.  
• Communications Officer - Manages Council communications, including newsletters, email updates, and social media.  
• Technology Officer - Supports the Council's digital needs, including assisting school administration with uploading Council minutes,  
agendas, and related updates to the school website.  
• Teaching and Non-Teaching Staff Representatives - Contribute insights from the school perspective.  
• Principal (or Vice-Principal) - Non-voting member who provides school updates and supports Council efforts.  
We hope to see many new and returning faces at our first meeting in September. Details will be shared with families closer to the date. Your  
involvement-whether through a formal role or by simply attending-is always welcomed and appreciated.  
Come out and see what Council is about!!  
Meetings will resume on the second Wednesday of every month, starting in October, and will include a virtual option. Everyone is welcome at any of  
the meetings (even just a meeting here and there)!
