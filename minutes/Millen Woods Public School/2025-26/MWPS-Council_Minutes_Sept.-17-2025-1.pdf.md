## Fall Fundraiser Brainstorming: All

We ran a survey last year regarding parents' feedback on fundraisers. The survey indicated that  
most families would rather make donations towards events (i.e. dance-a-thon, read-a-thon) instead  
of product sales.  
\- Brainstormed ideas for the fall fundraiser, which included:  
Fall festival - fundraising through the sale of apple cider, candy apples, face painting, etc.,  
can coordinate a farm donation for families to drop off pumpkins, and a community building  
event. Logistically, it could be difficult with the early November timing to run an outdoor  
event, and there may be no availability to book the gym.  
o Read-a-thon - this was well received when we ran it last time, but we would plan to do a  
shorter time frame (last time was a month - would be ideal to do a week or two). Can bring  
in new ideas such as dress as your favourite character day, sale of small products as part of  
it (stickers, bookmarks, buttons), can we bring back the book swap as part of it?  
• Coffee mornings - sell coffee at school drop off and allow parents to connect and chat with  
the community  
• Farm produce box - we have done this in the past and it was successful, but it was very  
difficult to find enough volunteers as it is very labour-intensive (sorting & handing out the  
boxes)  
\- A subcommittee was formed to discuss the ideas and come up with a fundraiser.

Mission Statement  
The Millen Woods School Council is a body of volunteers created to support and enhance learning for all students through the cooperative efforts  
of parents/guardians, students, staff members and others in the community."  
What We Do  
Here at Millen Woods, our School Council works with the school administration to organize fundraising efforts to support events and activities  
within the school. These events and activities help enrich what the amazing staff already does for our children.  
Some of the enrichment items include:  
• Additional classroom resources for children, such as decodable texts  
• Guest Speakers in the School  
Funding busing costs for classroom field trips and the Area Track & Field Meet  
• Nutrition Break Enrichment (playground equipment, maintenance and sports equipment)  
• Community Outreach Efforts (Winter Wonderland and Year-End Social)  
Technology Replacements in the Classroom not funded by the WRDSB (Projectors, Document Cameras)  
• Additional Kindergarten equipment and resources  
This year, we anticipate the installation of a Gaga Ball Pit, along with some new Kindergarten bicycles for the playground!  
As Council, we also organize Free Family Events at the school, such as Winter Wonderland and the Year-End Social.  
We also provide support, guidance and input for the administration on what we would like to see as a parent/caregiver body.  
Council meets once a month (on the 2nd Wednesday of the month) for approximately 1.5 hours, in person at the school library or via Google Meet.
