## Questions and Answers

● How do the results of current Grade 6 compare to when they were in Grade 3?  
○ No data for this. When they were in Grade 3 there was no EQAO due to COVID.  
● Does the school have access to individual students' results?  
○ Yes. The school is packaging up all the individual results and will then send  
out/release them to parents.  
● How can the school council support the EQAO with money for materials (games or tools)  
or programs ?  
○ The teachers are well resourced, it is more a question of how the resources can  
best be used. More important is to see which students are struggling and why and  
what do these students need to get them up to the standard. Also note that EQAO is  
only one data point that the school collects and uses.  
● Does the testing take into account neurodivergence?  
○ Not really. All kids are lumped together, however IEPs do refer to how to help kids  
do better in the EQAO.  
● Are there any EQAO prep programs offered at the school to help prepare them for tests?  
○ No. The EQAO is meant to show where the students are with regard to the  
standards and students are not required to pass the EQAO at this level. Teachers  
3  
are teaching to the EQAO standard everyday in class. An EQAO prep program  
could be exclusionary for families who don’t have the time or resources.  
● Are student behaviours impacting learning?  
○ Yes  
● Could the school provide parents with a list of games that could help?  
○ Yes and some teachers already do e.g. Spark reading, however some only work  
during school hours. There is the possibility that the school council could fund some  
games that could be checked out from the library.  
● Teachers are under-resourced and need more EA support. Is the board aware of the  
EQAO results?  
○ Yes the board and higher ups are aware of the struggles and are doing their best to  
resource and support schools with the money they have. The Chair advises that  
parents are able to request a question for the WRDSB meetings and can email  
trustees also.  
● What specific things is the school doing to address the EQAO results?  
○ The school has hired a math coach from the board to help Grades 3 and 6 teachers  
on how to apply math tools and lessons. This math coaching has seen an increase  
in previous schools results.  
○ A literacy coach will also be starting in the future.  
○ Coaches will work with both teachers and the students who need help.  
● Regarding the parking lot situation, is there any education focused on the parents as  
they are the ones driving?  
○ No specific education for the parents, instead information will be provided to the  
children to pass along to the family. Bylaw has been called on the parking lot  
recently, and at least one ticket and multiple warnings have been given out.
