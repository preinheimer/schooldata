## Safe Caring and Inclusive School (SCIS) Report

● Survey is done every spring and asks students about things like safety, bullying, sleep  
and connections with family and friends  
● Mr Wilson will prepare a report on the survey results and how to use the survey but is  
generally not content with the results of the survey  
● The school is already working on a SCIS committee to respond to the survey. It is  
strongly felt that the student voice is important, and a meeting has already occurred with  
18 students from GRs 7 and 8 to talk about their concerns  
● The student voice can offer simple solutions that have real a impact  
● Parent involvement is also being sought, please reach out to Mr Wilson if you would like  
to participate

discussions about these topics. All classes are represented by the students.
