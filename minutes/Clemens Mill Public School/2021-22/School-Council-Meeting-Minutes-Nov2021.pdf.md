○ Principal Tiwari clarified that students are not necessarily delayed as a result of the pandemic, but

○ Principal Tiwari and VP Sheriff were asked how their transition to Clemens has gone. Shemira  
shared that the community has been welcoming and kind, and that there is a noticeable change in  
students being more vocal and accustomed to being in school with all of the new pandemic-related  
restrictions. Principal Tiwari shared that his transition hasn’t been without its challenges - he  
learned of the news in mid-August and had to quickly close up his role at his old school and  
acclimate to Clemens. It is also his first time in Cambridge. He also agreed with Shemira that the  
community has been welcoming.  
○ What type of resources can parents use to get their kids up to speed? Advice is that globally, all  
children have been set back by the same pandemic. Parents are encouraged to read with their  
children, and not to worry about their kids being behind the curve because all students have been  
affected in the same way.  

## WRAPS-C MEETING UPDATE – A. Strickland

● Matt Gerard, Superintendent Business Service and Treasurer of the Board spoke about the PRO grant,  
intended to remove barriers to parent engagement. AIming to have information out to schools next week  
with applications due in mid-December. Also discussed fundraising in the COVID-era. Parents were  
requesting a loosening of restrictions, but it was still advised to utilize virtual methods and to engage with  
school principals to approve any plans.  
● Guest speaker from Carizon, Family Resource Coordinator Lead, discussed programs that families can  
access if needed. More information can be found here:  
https://padlet.com/eyoung106/Carizon\_Community\_Services\_Programs  
● Group discussion on equity and inclusion. How can we as Councils promote equity and inclusion. Some  
ideas shared were: communications in multiple languages (we can use School-Day functionality to achieve  
this), welcome packages to new families with school info (ie, start times, etc.)  
● Jackie asked for clarification of how the PRO grant will be managed - will it be a flat amount, or will it be  
awarded based on the cost of a specific item. Unknown at this stage. Also looking for clarity on how to  
qualify for the grant as the information that we have currently says that it can be used towards encouraging  
parent involvement and also promoting anti-racism/equity programs. Amanda advised that more information  
is to come.  
PROJECTS & FUNDRAISING UPDATE – J. Fraser, A. Rand
