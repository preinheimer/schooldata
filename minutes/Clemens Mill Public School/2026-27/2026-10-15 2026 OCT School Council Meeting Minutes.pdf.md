## Fiddlesticks Update

● Kim Fowler is the Executive Director of Fiddlesticks Community Centre. ● This year  
Fiddlesticks has been selected by TW Properties for a food donation partnership. For every  
1LB of donated food that Fiddlesticks receives, TW Properties will provide Fiddlesticks with  
\$1.  
● Fiddlesticks requests that any food donations raised by Clemens Mill be sent to  
Fiddlesticks to be part of this initiative. Fiddlesticks will take all donated food to TW  
Properties for weighing and processing.  
● At the end of the initiative Fiddlesticks will donate some equipment to the school using  
the funds it receives from TW Properties. They will also provide a pizza lunch to the  
school that donates the most food (by weight).  
● Clemens Mill Social Justice Committee to determine if they would like to partner with  
Fiddlesticks on this.
