## Welcome / Territory Acknowledgment

As we gather virtually this evening, we would like to honor the existence of the First  
Peoples by acknowledging that we are on the traditional land of the Anishinaabe,  
Haudenosaunee, and Mississauga peoples. New Dundee Public School occupies the  
land of the Between the Lakes Treaty from 1792. As learners, we continue to  
understand this place has significance to the First Peoples because of the rolling hills,  
forests, flat land, and the proximity to the Nith and Grand River, which is fed by the  
Great Lakes. Settlers need to take responsibility to continue our education and honor of  
the First Peoples. Let’s work to honor the Dish with One Spoon covenant, a peace  
agreement made between the Indigenous nations before the settlers arrived. It  
characterizes our collective responsibility to each other and to Mother Earth. We should  
only take what we need and leave enough for future generations.

## Care Committee

Dedicated to supporting the well-being of members through acts of kindness, empathy,  
and practical assistance. The committee's primary goal is to foster a compassionate  
environment where people feel cared for and supported during times of need, whether  
due to celebrations or personal challenges. Coordinating gestures: Sending cards,  
flowers, or small gifts to show care and support during life events and calendar  
celebrations. Collecting donations from the community is part of this role as it can not be
