## Principal report

● Report Cards and Communication of Learning will be sent home Feb 10, 2025  

● Mandatory Literacy Screening is in place this school year -- educators are  
communicating initial assessment results directly with caregivers, as well as on  
T1 Report cards. It is important to note that this is a snapshot from the beginning  
of the year. Teaching and learning continues, and this initial screening while  
included on the T1 report card in many cases is a different result to screenings  
that are occurring now.  

● Words Matter Campaign: Educator sessions have occurred each month,  
including framework for intervention, case studies, and resources to support  
educators and students during challenging discussions.  

● Literacy Lead Project: 4 educators have volunteered to take lead roles in the  
Literacy Lead Project this year -- attending professional development, and  
sharing learning and strategies back to their team. The first sharing session was  
in January, and will be following up with more substantial time during future staff  
meetings. Leads include Vernessa Kulikov (DECE, Jennifer Martin (K), Heidi  
Sproul (J) Rebecca Scott (I)  

● January 27 Math PD: 9 staff members volunteered to participate in Mathematics  
PD alongside Imbedded Itinerant staff. Focus is on the Mathematics Strategies  
detailed on the staff website, alongside the Tiered Intervention Strategies  
anchoring student support.
