Edna Staebler School Council Meeting Minutes  
Monday, January 23, 2023  

## Principal's Report - Susan Fryer-Davis

\- Teachers & teams are meeting to construct meaningful, planned wish lists on how  
they wish to spend the monies allotted for them (\$1200 for wish list items = \$50 per  
class)  
\- Pan Canadian Assessment Plan: our school has been randomly selected for this  
provincial assessment. 1 group of grade 8s will complete the assessment. Data is  
collected anonymously, and the assessment focuses on science, reading and math.  
\- EQAO testing dates will take place between May 10 and June 14. A new format was  
rolled out last year, and it rolled out well.  
\- Edna Staebler will partner with Laurelwood PS to offer a Social Media/Media Literacy  
Info session geared toward parents in February or March. This will be a virtual event,  
and the two schools will split the costs of this session (\$1200-\$1500). One school will  
host an in-person gathering so folks can participate in the session together if they  
choose, and it is hoped that a session for students (at least the intermediates) will take  
place during that same week.  
\- School Reaching Out Grant information has just been released. Applications are due  
next month.  
\- Grade 8 Graduation Photos are tomorrow!  
\- The Science of Reading program is underway and both French and English Teachers  
are collaborating to assess the resources available to support the science of reading in  
both streams. A consultant came in to help support teachers (especially French  
Immersion Teachers) review the resources available and how they are being used. Staff  
have found this helpful.  
\- Our score board is on the fritz, and  
) we are looking at how to get that fixed.
