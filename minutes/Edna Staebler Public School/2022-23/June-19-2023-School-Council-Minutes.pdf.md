## Nutrition for Learning Information

• 213 students served each day, this number had doubled. There is great need for this  
program.  
• 5 staff and many students help with this program.  
• Looking for parent volunteers next year.  
• Parent brought up concern that sometimes it feels like some kids are not eating their  
healthy lunches and instead just eating the granola bars from the program. Recognize  
that there is a tricky balance between making sure the program is open and comfortable  
for everyone while not being overused. Talked about implementing an education roll out  
next year (for example at an assembly) to remind kids the purpose of the program. If you  
are hungry then the program is there for you, but not if you just don't want to eat your  
lunch.

## Major council events next year

Bookfair (Oct), Moveathon (Feb), Spring Social (end of  
May), Steam fair (tbd)  
STEAM Fair was a huge success. We had over 50 projects presented with over 60 students  
participating (10% of the school) across grades 1 to 8. We had amazing community turnout for  
the event and inspired many students to participate next year. We were under-budget and spent  
less than \$500. Due to the cost of the poster-boards and anticipating increased participation, a  
larger budget will be needed for next year.  
Spring Social was another amazing event that was well attended. It was an extremely hot day,  
but everyone seemed to have a great time and was so excited to be together as a community  
post-covid. Ms. Craig's team of students did an amazing job running activities for the younger  
kids (we approved a \$100 pizza lunch to thank them for all their hard work). Cake walk and gifts  
baskets were hugely successful inside. DJ worked well outside as well. The movie attendance  
was only "ok" and few people stayed until the very end of it. Biggest complaint was the long food  
truck lines.
