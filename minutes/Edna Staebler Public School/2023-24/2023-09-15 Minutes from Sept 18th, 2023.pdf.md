\1. Edna Staebler School Council Meeting Minutes- September 18th, 2023  
Review of expense items approved from last year  
Social Emotional Learning was the Sara Westbrook presentation. This began with grades 1-8  
and will also begin with the Kindergarten and ACE classes in the near future.  
The grade ⅞ trip will occur in October. The school found that the monies allocated to subsidize  
trips in order to reduce barriers for students to attend, was not utilized last year. The hope is that  
by applying the money to the cost for all students will help families. Families that feel they are  
able to give more are welcome to donate to specific trips etc. through WEFI.  
Members would like to look at proceeding with the playground addition in the Spring. Looking to  
allocate \$15 000 additional to the \$11 000 previously approved. This will be revisited at the next  
meeting and voted on at a future time.
