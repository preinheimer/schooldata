6:50 - 6:55 pm WRAPSC update - Hilory Steffen attended and gave a summary of the  
last meeting.  
Hilory suggested that before the next WRAPSC meeting she can reach out to the council  
to ask if there are items that they would like added to the agenda to be discussed at  
WRAPSC.  
Re Halloween and the Board position – WRDSB acknowledges that they made a mistake  
in their messaging last year about Halloween. Board wants to do better and wants to  
make sure that messaging this year is about inclusivity. Students can definitely wear  
costumes if they choose. Board has not finalized their messaging for this year yet but stay  
tuned.  
Also discussed the idea of how can we support the different special days in the school  
year without it costing a lot for families eg. Halloween costumes, Orange Shirt Day.  
6:55 - 7:00 pm Kids Coat Exchange Update Wed. Oct 19th 3:30 - 4:30 pm  
Discussed the event this Wednesday. Everyone in the EZ community is welcome! Bring  
any coats/snow pants, boots, etc that you want to give away and feel free to take anything  
that is helpful for your family.  
Hilory brought up the idea of connecting with a “sister school” who might have the need  
for some of the clothing that is left over after our swap.  
7:00 - 7:10 pm Discussion/brainstorm event idea for PIC grant; Raising socially and  
emotionally healthy children  
Hilory is wanting to get us thinking about the PIC grant for this year, because the  
application will come out in December so it would be good to be prepared with some  
ideas.  
A big thing since the pandemic has been the mental and emotional health of the young  
people. Idea that the PIC grant could be used for something along these lines. Goal also  
would be that parents could connect in person at the school which we haven’t been able  
to do in previous years. Maybe something that involves some round table discussions  
which helps foster better connections.  
Anne suggested that if it is marketed as an opportunity for parents to connect with each  
other than this will be more appealing for parents. Calling it a parent social might help  
with turn out. And having child care is important so that families can come together.  
Hilory is suggesting doing a questionnaire to gauge what people’s area of interest are in  
the school community.  
Chantal told us about a program that she is involved with that provides STEAM activities  
for young people so maybe this free programming could be offered to the kids. This is  
called LAUNCH Waterloo.  
Maybe we could merge these ideas and have LAUNCH come and provide fun activities  
for the kids while the parents are doing something that is connecting them and engaging  
them and teaching them.  
7:10 - 7:20 pm Fundraising –  
Meaghan talked about Grocery Gift Cards and suggested that for the 5 Days of Giving

Teachers are asking for money for buses for field trips.

Farida suggesting that it is very important to have a purpose for each fundraising activity

Hilory is asking if the pizza volunteers would need to have the vulnerable sector check –  
Farida to check – answer in November meeting.  
7:20 - 7:30 pm Vice Principal/Principal report by Farida Jamal  
Outdoor Ed came in last week for puppet show and workshop for JK to grade 3 and also  
the ACE classes joined.  
Some clubs/activities have started – Running Club, Inter murals, Strong Start  
School Improvement and Equity Team is working on turning the celebrations of different  
cultures into a teaching/learning opportunity. For example, Diwali is coming up and there  
are students who have volunteered to go around to the different classrooms and teach  
other students about what this holiday means.  
There will be an education point for these cultural festivals so that students can learn  
about different cultures.  
Wanting to have an inclusive culture at the school around celebrations such as  
Halloween.
