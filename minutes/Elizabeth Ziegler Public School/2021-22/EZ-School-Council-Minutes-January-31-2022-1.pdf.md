## School Wishlist (Farida Jamal, Sara MacNeill)

Met with staff on Outdoor Learning Committee to ask what the needs for this  
program are. Staff wanted activity ideas for: forest, field, tarmac. Rebecca Seiling  
was consulted about ideas for activities in these zones that also address curriculum  
expectations in each grade. Rebecca is going to create a program for our school and  
then meet with staff by zoom to share what she comes up with. Estimating \$800 to  
create this resource for K-6. Wondering if council would be willing to contribute to  
this cost.  
Staff wanted to purchase materials such as tarps of different sizes, ropes, pulleys,  
compasses, thermometers, magnifying glasses – outdoor exploration tools. Idea is  
to store these somewhere central eg. In the gym storage so that it doesn’t take up  
space in the classroom. Classes would sign this out as needed. Looking for \$500 for  
this.  
Chef a l’ecole is a French language program based around cooking. Staff member  
applied for a grant and got \$600. Virtual presentation because of Covid. It would be  
for core French classes as well as French Immersion. For all 16 classes, \$1300 ish  
still required to cover this cost.  
Ryan is asking about whether or not field trips are possible this year. Sara says that  
there are lots of complications with transportation, volunteers. Currently the only field  
trips that are approved are the “class 1 field trips” which are walking field trips when  
there is the correct staff:student ratio.  
Jen and Carole both suggesting that in-person activities are preferable. Brenda  
sharing that she and her family did some Chef a L’ecole through the library and even  
though it was virtual it was great. Sara sharing that there are still many rules for  
guests to come into the school. Currently only essential visitors such as  
psychologists and speech pathologists etc. are allowed to come in to the school.  
Suggestion that we ask them if we book it for April and they are able to come in  
person at that time, can we switch it to in person? Sara to ask Madame Gauthier  
about this.

\5. Vice Principal/Principal general updates (Farida & Sara)  
Sara reached out to other school admin to discuss ideas of what people are doing  
for Fundraising at their schools. Dominoes Family Pizza Night – 4 week blitz where  
every Tuesday you get a code and if you go to Dominoes on University Ave and use  
the discount code – large 3 topping pizza for \$15 and our school gets \$5 for every  
pizza they sell. No limit, person doesn’t have to be directly connected to the school –  
we can share this code with anyone and people can use this online or in-person.  
Next round would start March 1 and would be for the month of March. Very little  
effort – just sending out the message on School Day and on FB to spread the word.  
Another idea is Factory Shoe Warehouse – same idea where the school gets a code  
and anyone who buys a pair of shoes over \$35 and uses our code we get \$5.  
Another idea is rain barrel fundraiser (rain barrels, composters) - \$55 for rain barrel,  
people order online and we pick a day and the items get dropped at the school and  
then people come and pick up what they ordered. You get \$10 for every rain barrel.  
They can also do home delivery but there is an extra fee of \$10 for that customer.  
These are very low effort ways to fundraise that are possible during Covid.  
Sara will send the info to the fundraising committee and also the greening  
committee.  
Today was photo day at EZ with LifeTouch. It was done following Covid protocols  
and was “no contact”. All students doing short-term virtual learning will have another  
opportunity this Spring on re-take day – likely end of May.  
We have registered for Feb 16th “Walk to School Day”. Students can register for the  
walk and have chance to win a \$20 gift card. Our EZ Trailblazers will be taking a  
leadership role on this day.  
Parents have been asking for clarification on parking. Allen St. on east side – there  
is a no parking sign and also a sign for school drop off. In this area of Allen St.,  
during school drop off time you can pull over and let your kids out but you cannot  
leave the car yourself. There is a new sign, No Stopping sign, opposite to bus zone  
on Moore St. This is for the safety of the kids who take the buses and there is no  
parking and no stopping in this area.  
We collected \$3695 from grocery gift card fundraiser this Fall and \$1290 has already  
been distributed. We will distribute more this week and then again before March  
Break. It would be good to do another grocery gift card fundraiser at end of March so  
that we have some gift cards to give out for the rest of the school year.
