## Role of School Council

Presented by Farida Jamal  
● Involvement of parents & guardians & wider community has positive impact on student  
academics and social development  
● Council is an advisory body – parameters set by ministery & board  
● Parents work with staff to help student learning – not a place for advocacy for one’s own child,  
should be dealt with through teacher & administration  
● Staff members of council (principal, vice principal and teachers) do not actually vote, cannot  
move or second other people’s motions

## Defining Executive Roles

Presented by Farida Jamal  

## Council Chair

o Chair is main liason with school, chairs the meetings, ensures that minutes are taken,  
default spokesperson for council  
o Elected by council – must be parent(s) who are not employed by school board (so cannot  
be held by principal, vice principal or teacher)  
o Voting member, has same duties as any other member AND chairs, prepares for  
meetings, sets agenda, communicates with principal on behalf of council  
o EZ chair has to manage the e-mail list for council members, liaises with EZ parent  
Facebook group. Prepares WRAPSC update – chair attends WRAPSC meeting, talks about  
it at next school council meeting  
o Approx 10 hours/month Job can be split between two parents, eases the workload  
(especially for coming to meetings). Sounds like a lot but is manageable.  

## Secretary

responsible for taking minutes & sharing with council & principal & finalizing them to  
upload  
o Keeps records of council  
o Tasks: record minutes & distribute for approval to exec. Send e-mail copies to approved  
minutes to larger council group. Follow-up with people who have other jobs. Available to  
answer e-mail from principal, vp and exec. Make sure someone else takes minutes if  
needed  
o 5-10 hours/month  

## Treasurer

draft budget, perform bookkeeping  
o Keep track of cash balance, provide monthy report, liaise with school secretary & be up  
to date on money  
o 5-10 hours/month.
