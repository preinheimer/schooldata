## Principal’s Report – Mr. Sendrea

• Apologies to everyone for delaying the October 20th meeting to today  
o Was on phone calls all night dealing with confirmed case of COVID-19  
• We had one case of COVID-19 confirmed which also caused issues with the bus  
• Following Sunday an additional case was confirmed and then another in the same class as the previous week  
• This caused an outbreak to be declared which caused the cohorts in the classroom and the bus to be  
dismissed for remote learning  
• As of today, all the teachers and children are back in the classroom  
• All the bussed students were brought into the gym to have a reminder about staying in your assigned seat,  
wearing your mask, and keeping the volume low  
• Question: Did the parents have complaints about confusion around messaging from Public Health?  
o Answer: Improvement is needed in the way communication flows. It flows from Public Health to the  
Board to the Principal and then to the parents

• There is a lot of traffic some days, but it is sporadic

Sawmill

• Cars do not generally follow the rules of the island divider, meaning they do not stop on both sides of the  
island when stopping for children/crossing guards
