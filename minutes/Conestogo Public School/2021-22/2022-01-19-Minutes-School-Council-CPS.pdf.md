## Teacher’s Report – Ms. Lacey

● Teachers are grateful to be back to in person learning  
● Online learning was challenging

● Question: What is the Kindergarten experience online?  
o K have a different set of minutes than primary (100 minutes)  
o Morning meet with all the students  
o Lessons and games synchronously  
o Difficult to have K students be independent with a computer for synchronous learning  

## Principal’s Report – Mr. Sendrea

• Online from Jan 4-14 by order of the Minister of Education  
• A number of changes to the way the school responds to Covid-19 cases  
o No longer reporting on numbers specifically  
o When there is a case in the school, the principal is no longer responsible for communicating to families  
o There are active cases right now but no reporting due to the changes  
o RAT were previously online for asymptomatic cases and are no valid for symptomatic cases
