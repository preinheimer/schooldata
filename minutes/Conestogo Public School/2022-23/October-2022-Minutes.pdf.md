## Teacher’s Report – Ms. Lori Hillman

● Ms. Hillman introduced herself and expressed appreciation for in person learning and her  
enthusiasm for teaching and learning in person for this school year.  
● Outdoor learning centre was discussed, including identifying tree species and the learning  
garden, indicating that outdoor learning is widely accepted amongst the students.  
● Gym equipment needed concerning deterioration of equipment. More gym equipment  
is required and replacements of existing gym accessories are needed.  
● Remembrance Day assembly on November 11, 2022, will be the very first assembly as a  
school community. This assembly solidifies and drives the spirit of community within the  
school. Letters are written to veterans in nursing homes and there will be a Google Doc  
created to commemorate this observance.  

## Principal’s Report – Mrs. MacNeill

● Outdoor Education (Land Based Learning)  
o Outdoor Education team from the Waterloo Region District School Board (WRDSB)  
Outdoor Ed centre came out to do some outdoor activities and learning with K‐6  
students.  
o There are 2 Monarch Waystations and the students will be planting seeds to  
create biodiversity  
o Land based learning and our outdoor space will be utilized by staff and students  
to enhance learning throughout the school year in all kinds of weather and  
children should be dressed accordingly.  
● Student Leadership  
o Students have been eager to help and volunteer their time in all requested areas.  
o Grade 5 & 6 students are volunteering their time as ‘Lunch Helpers’ for the  
primary grades.  
o Grade 7 & 8 students have started a ‘Leadership Team’ wherein they will be  
involved in school assemblies, MCing for Spirit Days, class visits, announcements,  
making posters, having a tech team for setting up sound systems for assemblies,  
etc. This leadership aspect will be evolving as the students want to be involved  
and contribute to school culture. They meet every Tuesday and Mrs. Thiel and  
Mrs. Fox are helping facilitate this team.

## Playground Update – Colleen Heinrichs

● The playground structure and installation discrepancies continue to persist. Alex Ramirez,  
Facility Manager, for the Board of Education, compared the structure.  
● Parent Council was advised to request that the vendor provide specs and installation  
requirements. No specs were provided.  
● The vendor appears to be ‘pushing back’ indicating that it may be the mulch or the grading  
of the land. They have offered to come out to discuss the installation.  
● Based on the structure in Williamsburg and compared to our structure, the difference in  
elevation for key aspects such as the ‘monkey bars’ is anywhere from 6” to 12”, based on  
measurements from a ‘lay’ person.  
● The new play structure was also the wrong colour. There needs to be leeway to negotiate,  
whether it be requesting a refund or acquiring more useful play equipment.
