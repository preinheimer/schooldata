## Teacher’s Report – Mrs. Fox

● Staff meeting was held outside for a ‘change of pace’  
● Exploring ways to incorporate indigenous education to maintain students’ interest by  
relating it to their own lives  
● Focus on nature/indigenous perspective and connecting it to language, science and math  
● Introduce literature, art and music by indigenous cultures, i.e. “The Giving Tree” in Metis  
culture  
● WRDSB will be rolling out a reading and writing program based on the Hagerty Program.  
Science is proving that learning how to read isn’t a natural process.  
● The importance of phonics was highlighted at the staff meeting. Phonics is the pathway  
to reading and whole group instruction will be starting in JK and the program will be made  
available for the primary and junior grades. Strong Start pairs well with the Hagerty  
program.  
● There has been a cycle of peak absences within the last couple of months due to various  
colds and viruses being spread. Teachers need to come up with good ways to keep  
everyone connected.

## PLAYGROUND UPDATE (Added to Agenda) – Colleen Heinrichs

● There has been acknowledgment that the Williamsburg playground and our newly  
installed playground have discrepancies with the installation. The vendor will attempt  
to remedy the situation in the spring of 2023.

required approvals will be fast tracked.

monetary compensation.
