## Teacher’s Report – Mrs. Fox

 Kindergarten Class, Storage bins are required. Going to request that Parents of the  
children within the classroom donate storage bins. Size requests to be determined and  
bins must be clear (see through) with a lid.  
 Grade 4/5 Reading Hannah’s Suitcase. Holocaust study. Learning about coding and  
making up games with coding.  
 Grade 7 – Mr. Lamber has opened the woodshop with a plan to make interactive toys for  
the Kindergarten class  
 Grade 1 – Building bridges. Mark Edwards will be helping with the bridge construction.  
 Grade 3 – Ms. Brown is preparing her class for Grade 3 testing.  
 Ms. Carere organized a trip to the symphony. The kids enjoyed the field trip. She is also  
planning an interactive musical experience using percussion. Ms. Fox is doing something  
similar with sticks and a sign language song with an Indigenous theme.  
 LIBRARY – last day to check out books will be June 12, 2023 for the school year.  
 Ms. Hillman teaching about pulleys and gears  
 Wendy Rudd, Special Education Resource Teacher, will be supporting ‘Hagerty’. Math  
and Language will be the focus. Minister of Education, Stephen Lecce, has a program  
tracking the progress of students in Gr. 1‐3 with the goal of having less children needing  
intervention in the junior grades  
 Ms. Fox is Retiring. She has taught for 20 years at CPS and going forward she intends to  
support the Hagerty program and join the Supply List. She is interested in physical  
education, volunteering. She is very grateful for all of the support she has received at this  
school during her tenure.
