## Teacher’s Report – Ms. Cochrane

● Pilot project for coding. Our school was gifted 15 Microbits. Grade 7s have started  
working with them. This technology is being introduced in the classroom for science.  
● Youth Climate Action. A conference is being held at the University of Waterloo and 4  
students from Grade 7 will be attending on Tuesday, October 10, 2023. The school has  
been gifted \$500.00 for their Action plan.  

## Principal’s Report – Mrs. MacNeill

● Library – Air Conditioning has been installed and new storage units are required for  
book storage and will be installed.  
● Spirit Day: November 3 – Mustache Day  
● Grade 4 – 6: Lunch Helping  
● Grade 7 – 8: Leadership Team organizing Terry Fox Day, Spirit Day.  
● Grade 6 – 8: Trailblazers’ are end of day Kindergarten helpers and bus patrollers.  
● Grade 5 – 8: Ms. Theil, Cross Country meet (Grade 7/8) at Centennial school in  
Waterloo on Wednesday, October 4, 2023. There will be a Junior Cross Country meet for  
Grades 5/6.  
● Volleyball: Will be starting school. Release dates will be posted, as no one is allowed  
to practice early.  

## Volunteers

Close to 50 volunteers are registered with the school  
● Child and Youth Worker: Support is being offered to children for social support, personal  
regulation, emotional learning and social awareness. Mrs. Winger is allocated to our  
school and has 20+ years as a child and youth worker. She is scheduled to come on a ½  
day a week or on an as need basis. She is not a counsellor. The focus is on the  
social/emotional needs of the child.  
● Occupational Therapist: Work with classroom teachers, self‐regulation, sensory, gross  
motor skills. To give teachers suggestions for the classroom and/or whole class strategies.  
● Math Intervention Program: Ms. Theil & Ms. Rudd applied for and were approved for  
this math intervention program. The focus is to address gaps in math. It is a 3‐tier model.  
First Tier is a whole classroom instruction, Second Tier involves small group instruction  
and Third Tier is more of an intervention whereby a student(s) will be withdrawn from  
other subjects to focus on individual math instruction. Chad is the Math Intervention  
Specialist. Parents will be notified if their child falls under Tier 3. He is here until March,  
and he comes to the school 2x’s per week. This initiative is to address math and prepare  
them for entering high school.

## PLAYGROUND

● Will remain as it is. There are no more avenues with respect to adjusting the structure.  
Financial reimbursement with respect to the wrong colour. Funds will have to be given  
through WEFI. The structural integrity of the playground is in question as there are certain  
areas that appear ‘loose’.  

## INQUIRY ITEMS

SPIRIT WEAR – This is a fundraiser. Opportunity to purchase clothing items (joggers,  
sweatshirt, t‐shirts) with the school logo. This is primarily directed towards the older grades as  
they have expressed an interest in purchasing school ‘swag’ to commemorate their time at  
school. More concrete information is required, such as cost and how many items will be offered.  
To be placed on Agenda for our next meeting.  
ESNS – School Lunch program to expand our current lunch ordering options. All funds  
raised will support Parent Council’s budget. ESNS offerings need to meet inclusive cultural and  
dietary requirements which may be an issue.  
COMMEMORATIVE TREE – Interest within the school community to plant a tree for  
retired teacher Ms. Fox. We have made an inquiry as to what types of trees would be available  
for planting. We as Parent Council need to formalize criteria as to which retiring staff member(s)  
qualify for commemorative trees in the future.
