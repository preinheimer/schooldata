PRESENTATIONS  

## Teacher’s Report – Mrs. Fox

● Jump Rope for Heart to take place on June 16, 2023  
● Skating Program with Skate Canada took place at RIM Park over the course of 4 days.  
Transportation was organized amongst parents. This was a positive experience for all for  
all participants. Only available to Grade 1.  
● LIDS FOR KIDS – There will be a presentation from Lids for Kids to stress the importance  
of wearing a bike helmet. The presentation is reflective and geared towards the different  
Grades within the school.  
● Farm Safety Information (Rural School) – A presentation was given by a grandmother of  
a Grade 1 student. A lamb was brought in, and the students were enthralled and visited  
the kindergarten classes as well.  

## Principal’s Report – Mrs. MacNeill

● Track & Field – Regionals for Grade 7/8 to take place June 23rd. Grades 4‐6 are held on  
June 19, 2023. Timing of the events is a problem with respect to transportation.  
● Parent Workshop – This is a literacy workshop for the primary and junior grades.  
Information regarding ‘Decodables’ will be discussed.  
● EQAO – Grade 3 starts on May 23 – 26. Grade 6 starts on June 5 – June 9.  
● Yearbooks available to order through School Day at \$10.00  
● May 18, 2023 – Grade 7 Emergency Preparedness Field Trip  
● Grade 8 YMCA 3 Rivers outdoor adventure trip to take place May 25, 2023. Parent  
Council contributed \$170 towards the cost of the trip in order to bring the cost down to  
\$39/student.  
● Grade 6 – 8, Ms. Carere is organizing bucket drumming to take place May 30, 2023  
● Grade 8 – On May 31 attending Elmira District Secondary School to familiarize  
themselves with the school.  
● June 1, 2023 – Rainbow Spirit Day  
● June 2, 2023 – PD Day  
● June 23, 2023 – The staff has decided to run a play day and Grades 6‐8 will help  
facilitate.  
● KINDNESS CLUB, The Safe Caring and Inclusive Schools Team meet every other Monday.  
Activities include Human Bingo, Bucket Filling, Kahoot game on kindness. On May 29,  
2023, an Assembly will be organized to explain the Kindness Club to the primary and  
junior grades.  
● Library – Magazine subscriptions would be greatly appreciated for the library. The  
anticipated cost would be around \$200 and Parent Council would be requested to fund  
this request. This will be a ‘wish list’ item for the 2023‐2024 school year.
