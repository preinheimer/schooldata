## Playground Planning Update

● Facilities manager came out and looked at things with Brian  
● What they (School Board) are doing now is asking schools “How much money do you  
have?”  
. Based on this answer, they put out requests for bids based on the amount of  
money available to be spent  
● One thing that could be done is that part of it could be removed and replaced!  
● All playgrounds now that are built HAVE to be accessible; we could promote it as an  
accessible addition to what we have - it would be designed in a way that would be  
multi-functional; this would bring more purpose and would be a positive thing for our  
community  
● In order to this we would have to also change the surface, remove some of the outside  
barrier to allow access  
● To do this option we would be looking at a minimum of \$40,000 - \$50,000  
● We will look at a committee to focus on this in the fall as part of a multi-year project
