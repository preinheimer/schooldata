A school council is an advisory body that functions within the parameters of Ministry of Education and Board policy.  
School councils provide an opportunity and the means to advise the principal and the Board on any matter pertaining to  
improvement of pupil achievement and enhancing the accountability of the education system to parents/guardians. The  
ultimate responsibility for the administration and management of the day-to-day operation of the school rests with the  
principal.

Silverheights PS piloted an anonymous bullying reporting tool.  
Instructions for use shared from the Board was not prescriptive and  
few community reports were submitted by Silverheights PS,  
representing one of the lowest rates of incidents and the absolute  
lowest by percentage of population. School admin attributes this to  
consistent character education and by holding kids accountable and to  
ensure they develop from a learning perspective. School admin  
recognizes (the low rate) does not mean there is not bullying but they  
try hard to bring awareness to it. Silverheights Bullying Prevention &  
Intervention Website This link is a tool where you report an element of  
bullying if you are a student, caregiver, teacher etc.  
\-  
Continued focus areas from school improvement perspective we will  
continue with literacy and inclusivity, culture creed and neurodiversity  
\-  
School admin has asked teachers to create a learning goal and create  
a plan for themselves which will directly affect the students learning  
and wellbeing  
\-  
Safety drills at the school include firedrills, extreme weather (e.g.  
tornado) drills, and lockdown (hold and secure). Ongoing learnings  
from consecutive firedrills (total of three thus far). Latest fire drill was  
perfectly executed. PA system broadcasts these drills to the school  
exterior so community members who overhear these drills should not  
be alarmed or get involved in any way. Established policies and  
procedures by the Ministry and Board in the event of actual incidences  
are at this link: Administrative Procedure 1360  
\-  
Lots of school transportation (bussing) issues to start the year. The  
school does not have control over these issues; it is run by  
Board-approved transportation company. The Board is doing what they  
can to sort this out.  
\-  
Acknowledgement to 2024-2025 School Council for taking the initiative  
as caregivers to advocate for additional traffic calming and control  
measures for the safety of our walking students. Specifically, a  
crossing guard is now posted at Sasha/Poplar to help students cross  
the road & this has helped the flow of walkers considerably  
\-  
2 City Bylaw blitzes have been completed since the beginning of the  
school year. 36 tickets given on the third day of school.  
\-  
Per Ministry mandate related to protection of daycare facilities from  
vehicular incidence, safety pylons have been placed in the parking lot  
of the licensed daycare area. These are a temporary measure until  
the Ministry provides specific guidance and funding for permanent  
solutions.
