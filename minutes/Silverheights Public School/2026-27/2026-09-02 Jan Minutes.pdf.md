A school council is an advisory body that functions within the parameters of Ministry of Education and Board policy.  
School councils provide an opportunity and the means to advise the principal and the Board on any matter pertaining to  
improvement of pupil achievement and enhancing the accountability of the education system to parents/guardians. The  
ultimate responsibility for the administration and management of the day-to-day operation of the school rests with the  
principal.

Pizza Volunteer members picking up pizza for fellow School  

## Council members

● If a pizza volunteer member knows that a fellow member's  
child is absent, and they want to pick up their pizza, and they are on  
the list, they are welcomed to do so. School Council members and  
pizza volunteers are expected to follow the same guidelines as the rest  
of our school population.  
Key points if students are NOT on the ordered list:  
● Students who are not on the pizza order list as having ordered  
pizza but claim they have, are to be directed to the main office for  
support. There are many nuances to these situations that involve  
privacy, safety and liability issues with students. Sarah will seek  
assistance from Tara/Jason with complex cases. Mistakes made by  
our community, by the ordering software or by the school, fall on the  
shoulders of Mr. Stere - School Council/Pizza Volunteers are not  
expected to deal with these.  
● We do NOT use past history/patterns of ordering in our  
decision making for the present week in question.  
● Students in conflict over pizza are to be directed to the main  
office.  
Each week may present new and unique challenges. Tara and Jason  
will deal with those challenges in the main office, in the moment, with  
each child/family's best interest in mind. Our approaches may differ,  
but rest assured that we chat daily and we trust one another in their  
decisions. We use a fairness model - which differs from sameness or  
equal.  
In Elementary School we use a saying,  
"Keep Small Problems Small  
and seek help to solve"  
Our goal is always to simplify your role as pizza volunteers so you  
continue to enjoy coming here and interacting with our community. We  
definitely enjoy having you here.  
If you have any questions or concerns, you are welcome to call Jason  
or Tara and we will be happy to chat with you.
