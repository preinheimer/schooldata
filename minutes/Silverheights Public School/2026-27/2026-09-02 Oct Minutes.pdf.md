## 5 Principal’s Report

● Progress reports underway; these interim reports are different from  
formal report card gradings.  
● “Be Kind” initiative with the educators & admin team underway to  
address behaviours in classroom and among students. Educators are  
identifying a lack of understanding & definition of “bullying”  
,  
micro-aggressions, vs disrespectful behaviour. Staff are being  
challenged to define,  
“what does kindness mean to the students?”  
○  
Emotional toolkit - students contribute to a classroom "kit" that  
could include fidgets, comfort textures, mindfulness/calming  
materials, connection tools etc.  
○  
● Educators are requesting ideas from parents to engage in this initiative  
for next Council meeting. Silverheights PS may request support from  
Council in the form of financial or other supports, to be discussed at  
next Council meeting.  
● Potential for strike action by part-time staff at the daycare and Youth  
Development Program (YDP) run by Conestoga College in the  
Silverheights building (southeast portion of building). Communications  
from union and Conestoga College will be shared via School Day. The  
daycare and YDP will close if work action occurs & any worker protest  
to be limited to public area (sidewalk) and not on school property.
