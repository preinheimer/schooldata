## Facility Updates

Flood- theme of the year is plumbing, still continues  
to be an issue, removal of water in various forms has been extremely  
problematic. Result of the floods recently is a real disrespect of the  
actual facilities by the students, still having issues with this, kids who  
are habitually vandalizing the washrooms which is costing a lot of  
money to the school and board and the pressure on our custodial staff  
is immense. Difficult to monitor student ages & discipline due to the  
nature of the vandalism. Message to school community pending via  
School Day to notify families/caregivers. This is the worst year for  
these occurrences. Ramifications are financial, from a plumbing and  
sanitation standpoint.
