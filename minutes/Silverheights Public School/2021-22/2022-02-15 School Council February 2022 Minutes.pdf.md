## Equity Connections

\- Snow Day – new practice is that students log  
in and complete asynchronous learning;  
equity consideration is for effort from teachers  
to support students without technology. Within  
90 minutes chromebooks were sent home to  
students that needed them. Well done staff to  
prep students to be able to participate.  
\- Black Brilliance Month – some decisions have  
been made to reflect Black Brilliance (not  
Black History Month) to celebrate  
accomplishments, not history. Discussions  
need to be done very … carefully … given  
current climate. Discussions are being done  
locally within individual classrooms. No large  
scale assemblies. Focus is on student lead  
initiatives.  
\- Days of observance for cultural celebrations  
(such as Lunar New Year, etc.) ? There are  
many days of observance given the large  
diversity of our student population. Staff are  
all given a calendar of important events and  
are encouraged to celebrate in their  
classrooms. On average there are 7 – 8 days  
in EACH week that are important days of  
observance in various cultures/faiths in our  
community. Based on classroom  
representation will be encouraged, not  
necessarily school-wide initiative.

## PIC Grant

All School Councils automatically receive  
\$500 from the MOE for events or activities to  
“engage parents in their child’s education.  
”  
Funds must be spent between Sept. 1 & June  
\30.  

## PRO Grant

These grants encourage parent engagement  
at the local, regional & provincial levels & are  
designed to support parents in identifying  
barriers to parent engagement in their own  
community & to find local solutions to involve  
more parents in support of student  
achievement & well-being.

## 8 New Business

Supportive protest planned on Friday to support  
the truckers “Freedom Convey”  
\- No disciplinary action is taken for the protest  
\- If kids take the protest outside the building,  
this poses more difficulty for the school to  
ensure student safety.  
\- Easier to manage if kids keep the protest  
indoors within the building.  
\- Typically junior and intermediate; plan will be  
put in place (as best as possible) for Friday  
for flexibility. Staff will be available.  
\- Protest behavior is ‘welcome’ but  
disrespectful behavior is not tolerated. Also  
need to ensure that students who are not  
protesting still have access to curriculum,  
within Covid protocols.  
\- Student safety will not be compromised under  
any circumstances.
