## 3 Principal’s Report

\- Read aloud: Rain before Rainbows (David Litchfield,  
https://www.amazon.ca/Rain-Before-Rainbows-Smriti-  
Prasadam-Halls/dp/1536212830)  
\- Update on cyber attack to WRDSB has left lingering IT  
issues affecting day-to-day administration, access to  
education resources, & use of School Day for general  
communications.  
\- Manual logging of attendance by 10am by admin  
\- Main form of communication from the school will come  
through School Day; registered users still able to receive  
& access functions but new families unable to register  
onto School Day at this time. To be resolved soon.  
\- Individual teachers are using different platforms for online  
resources (some using Google Classroom, website, or  
Twitter) but are not mandated to maintain.  
\- School Reorganization: Registered enrollment end of  
August 800+ students with 37+ on cultural leave and 15+  
absent due to COVID screening during the first week of  
September. Based on provincial criteria, Silverheights  
lost 1 educator. Students on cultural leave to return mid-  
October but no reorg will take place; students to remain  
in current classrooms but may have increased number of  
students.  
\- Professional Development: Theme of inclusion & equity  
to continue  
\- Kindie classroom emphasis on self-regulation  
\- Junior classroom emphasis on small math group  
instruction  
\- Intermediate classroom emphasis on increasing  
resiliency, addressing procrastination & learned  
helplessness, & building social networks.  
15 mins Principal (Jason  
Stere)  
\- COVID Protocols: Optional masks, continuing increased  
touch-point cleaning, providing sanitizer in classrooms,  
HEPA filter units located throughout the school & in all  
common areas (provided by WEFI funding & by the  
Province).  
\- Library renovation planned in summer 2023 to revitalize  
the space as the new Learning Commons. Renovation  
includes room across the hall to become the ortho-  
sensory room. Planning underway.  
\- Successful Meet the Teacher night on Sept 20.  
Feedback from the Council table & parents provided to  
teacher organizers. Participating food trucks to donate to  
Silverheights through WEFI.
