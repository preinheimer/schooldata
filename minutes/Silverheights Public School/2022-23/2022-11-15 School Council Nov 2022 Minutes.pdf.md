 deal with islamophobia, racism (ie. anti-black, anti-asian, antisemitism, xenophobia)

 reporting line: purpose is to help the city understand the extent of violence within the community,  
which will lead to the development prevention programs and services. They also want to find out  
where it is taking place, to find out who has been impacted. For the victims: to access supports, to  
educate, to provide counselling referrals and mental health supports, and to provide a link to police  
services  
 Education: deliver education sessions on hate, discrimination, racism, and active bystander training to  
school staff. Might also come to speak to school councils.  
 Terms  
o Hate incidents: cannot be treated legally, but impactful to the victim  
o Hate crime: offense specifically designated in the Criminal Code of Canada with an aspect of  
hate: a hate-motivated crime ( ie property damage and assault)  
o Hate speech: verbal, online, or print messaging that encourages hatred & may lead to  
personal threats
