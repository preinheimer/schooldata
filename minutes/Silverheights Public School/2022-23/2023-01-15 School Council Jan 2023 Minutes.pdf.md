PRO Grant application process  
The application process for the 2022-23 Parents Reaching Out (PRO) Grant for School Projects  
is now open.  
Overview  
The Parents Reaching Out (PRO) Grants provides funding for school boards to work with their  
Parent Involvement Committee and/or school councils to lead initiatives that address local  
parent needs and/or remove barriers that prevent parents from participating and engaging fully  
in their children’s learning and educational progress.  
PRO funding is intended to be used by local communities to identify and address barriers to  
parent engagement. Projects that support the diversity of parent needs and parent communities  
at the local level through an equity and inclusion lens are eligible for PRO funding.  
Ministry Criteria for Funding  
PRO funding continues to support initiatives that remove barriers to parent engagement in their  
children’s education. Projects that demonstrate how schools can improve in at least one of the  
following areas are eligible for funding:  
● Addressing social inclusion and racism specifically anti-Asian, anti-Black, and anti-  
o Indigenous racism and other prejudices toward racialized and marginalized groups  
● Creating a safe and welcoming environment  
● Demonstrating respect for parents as valued partners within the education system regarding  
decisions about their child’s education  
● Enhancing communication with teachers  
● Informing parents about the expectations of the Ontario Curriculum and supporting  
resources.  
Examples of projects shared by PICs and Board Leads from the 2021-22 school year include  
but were not limited to:  
● Anti-Black Racism, anti-Indigenous and anti-oppression initiatives  
● Parent/caregiver engagement supports (e.g., evening conferences)  
● Enhanced multi-lingual and technology support (e.g., virtual presentations, online  
networking)  
● Food sovereignty and environment (e.g., hiking, community gardens);  
● Mental health and well-being (e.g. workshops on student and family mental health)  
● Physical health and wellness (e.g. Zumba)  
● Providing targeted resources to meet specific parent needs so parents can be resilient (e.g.,  
parent curriculum-briefing workshops)  
● 2SLGBTQI (e.g., awareness building by offering culturally responsive experiences)  
● STEM (e.g. in-person learning nights)  
● Online learning software and safety (e.g., cyber-literacy); and,  
● Literacy supports for parents to help their child(ren).  
Ineligible Project Costs:  
● Activities that took place prior to September 6, 2022  
● Payment to school board staff or volunteers including salaries, honoraria, gifts  
● Refreshments exceeding 10% of approved funding  
● Out-of-province travel  
● Debts or financial losses that result from a project

Application Process & Deadlines  
The Waterloo Region District School Board will accept and evaluate PRO Grant  
applications. Please complete the appropriate google form application below to apply for a PRO  
Grant.  
Elementary School PRO Grant Project - 2022-23 PRO Grant Application Form  
Secondary School PRO Grant Project - 2021-22 PRO Grant Application Form  
The deadline for submitting a 2022-23 PRO Grant application is Friday, February 24, 2023.  
PRO Grant funding allocations by school will be communicated directly to  
Administrators in a subsequent email.  
To be eligible for PRO Grant funding, projects must be completed and expenses incurred  
between September 6, 2022 and June 16, 2023.  
A final report is to be completed and submitted to rose  
\_  
dimartino@wrdsb.ca by June 23, 2023.  
PRO Grant recipients are encouraged to submit their final report as soon as their project is  
completed.  
PRO Grant Evaluation Committee includes representation from the Parent Involvement  
Committee, Special Education Advisory Committee, Equity and Inclusion Advisory Group and  
Board of Trustees.  
PRO Grant School Projects must be planned to engage parents in a way that adheres to current  
Region of Waterloo Public Health and WRDSB COVID-19 Guidelines.
