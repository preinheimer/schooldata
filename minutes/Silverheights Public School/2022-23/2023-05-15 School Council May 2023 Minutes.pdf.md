● Set the tone for the rest of the year at the first meeting:  
o set goals and objectives of the council, so we have a plan for our year that helps keep us  
focussed.  
o review major items from the past year and goals for the new year  
o How we hope to work together and that open and honest discussion with the goal of  
enhancing the experience for the kids as a whole. Elects other voting members. Picks  
day, time and frequency for meetings. Sets expectations for timing of budget.  
o explain role of council - citing the Ontario code, explain that primary function is NOT  
fundraising, explain how decisions are reached, explain quorum and attendance,  
highlight accomplishments of the previous year  
o Introductions and whose kids are in what grade  
o share an info packet ahead including roles & responsibilities (taken from the handbook)  
and events/fundraising some in the past, our councils purpose, etc.  
o Usually it is explained what it means to be a “member at large” (voting member) and the  
concept of motions and votes are explained.  
o I ask the council what they would like to see on the agenda for the year. I also tell them  
about all the free talks that are available for parents in the region. I give them the links  
and general dates and times. I give them any updates on any projects that were in the  
works last year, and where the project stands now, like getting a crosswalk at our school.  
o We discuss the importance of quorum. We also plan out how many meetings we will  
have that school year, and leave some as optional ie. March and June.  
o do a recap of activities, events and fundraising from the previous year and highlight the  
major year over year events already on the calendar.
