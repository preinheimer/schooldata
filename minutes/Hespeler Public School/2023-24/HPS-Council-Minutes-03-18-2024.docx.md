Gardening - Alicia  
	•	Mary Young (Alicia’s mom, 60+ year gardener) has proposed a multi-day approach to rejuvenate the flower beds.  
	•	Spring 2024 clean-up day in early April: rake mulch off of current flower beds and use it to top up around the trees and sign; fix the shape of the mulch around trees, edge flower beds, dig out flower bed weeds, add 2-3” of composted manure on all flower beds.  
	•	Rebecca will find out if we can use industrial vinegar (10% acetic acid) to help eliminate the crown vetch (very invasive weed).  
	•	If purchased composted manure & mulch must come from an approved vendor (or someone can apply to become an approved vendor), if donated it can come from a non-approved vendor. Unfortunately the free mulch from the city has a lot of weeds & diseased plants in it, so not good for making the gardens easier to maintain.  
	•	A 2nd spring 2024 date will be needed to transplant existing plants, plant new ones & add new mulch.  
	•	Fall 2024 students could help to remove weeds, plant more bulbs, add mulch if needed. Parents could cut back plants as needed before winter.  
	•	Spring 2025 could plan more perennials if needed, dig out weeds.  
	•	Alicia would like to increase the gardening budget by at least \$500 to cover costs: 3 yards of composted manure \$650, 4-6 yards of mulch \$475, compost bags \$50, bulbs/plants/fertilizer \$275.  
	•	Chris motioned to increase the garden budget by \$1000 (to bring total to \$1500), Tara seconded, approved unanimously.  
	•	Create a garden subcommittee to make decisions outside of main meetings, and report back to the group (Alicia, Emily, Darcie, Carra, Cassie).  
	•	Alicia/Emily will draft a post for School-Day to create interest in volunteers for gardening day on April 13, also create a sign.

## Further discussion/questions came up

	•	What is the purpose of the structure and does this spot meet those needs? Would it be used for more purposes if it’s not a concrete floor? If it’s concrete, do we need to provide tables? Might be used for more options if base is grass? Maybe we do grass now and reevaluate if needed?

	•	Kids consistently look for shade at breaks, it’s quite hot May-June/Sept-Oct, and then community use during the summer, a lot of which is our school’s families.  
	•	The cost is high, is this where we spend all the money we have saved? It has been something that’s been in discussion for a number of years. Other needs will still get covered, it’s about saving fund for this.

Rubberized is equally expensive and there are maintenance issues with it from facilities perspective.
