## Principal/Vice-Principal Update

## Principal’s Update – Rebecca Jutzi

	•	Kindergarten registration is open for kids born in 2020  
	•	French Immersion registration is open for gr1 students until Jan. 31 since FI now starts in gr2. We put out a letter online to help parents have a clear understanding of what the program looks like and to help them make the decision. 58% of the day is in French (gr2-6) (except for math, science & language/English). Gr7/8 has 50% of the day in French. It doesn’t leave a lot of time for English language instruction. For some kids, this works fine. For others, this isn’t enough time in language and then FI might not be the right choice. Reach out to your child’s homeroom teacher, their core French teacher or admin for help deciding if it’s a good fit for your student. The board doesn’t want the default choice to be FI to try it and then have students leave a few years in. Moving forward, the board wants the school to get superintendent consultation before moving a student from FI to English.  
	•	High school registration is in the works. Magnet program applications were due Jan. 12.  
	•	Teachers are working on term 1 report cards. New this year is that English language is combined into 1 mark instead of being 4 marks. There may be a lot of questions on reading vs. writing so please have a conversation with the teachers if you would like specifics. We can’t have parent teacher interview nights. You can see the new curriculum on the Ontario education website.  
	•	Jan. 19 PD Day is for report card writing.  
	•	Mad Science is coming back for gr1-6. It will be Mondays from 3-4pm. We had an assembly today to introduce it. This is an optional paid program.  
	•	Hot Shot martial arts is offering a parents night as a fundraiser for the school. You can register to drop kids off and they can try out martial arts.  
	•	We are having the parking signage assessed as it’s not clear in some places, so it’s a work in progress. We should include signage on Foxridge Dr. as well as in front of the school. The city will not allow us to have a kiss & drop because we don’t have the space needed.  
	•	We had White Hatters do live virtual presentations on digital literacy. Older students need reminders of how to conduct themselves online!
