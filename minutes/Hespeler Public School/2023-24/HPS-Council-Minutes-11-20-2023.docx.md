## Principal’s Update – Rebecca Jutzi

	•	Report cards were sent home today. Feel free to set up a meeting with your child’s teacher if you have anything you’d like to discuss. There is no formal day for interviews.

	•	 Social media continues to be a concern with students. We have White Hatter (from Victoria, B.C.) booked on Dec. 8 for live virtual 75-minute presentations (gr4/5, gr6-8) about social media safety, privacy, etc.  
	•	High school visits are coming up for gr8’s, along with information about magnet programs offered. Parent nights also happen for all the high schools.  
	•	We again have a possibility for intermediate students (gr7/8) to go to a Blue Jay’s baseball game in the spring (April). We do subsidize families who ask for help. Admin is open to feedback on this, bussing costs are going up & up. Rebecca would rather not ask for more than \$50 per student for a school trip.  
	•	The current “school” game day happens to be on our PD Day. So, this doesn’t work for us. They would then have to go to a 3pm game on a different day, and the tickets cost more. Busses are \$1600 per bus x3 busses.  
	•	Would council support \$8 per student for 192 students? To bring the cost down to \$50 per student.  
	•	Are we hoping to continue offering this trip in the coming years as something that will continue to benefit students? This is a trip that the PE teachers organize voluntarily. It may change over the years, but council could support other things that may happen in the future too.  
	•	Jill motioned to allocate \$2000 to the intermediate field trip this year. Jesse seconded, approved unanimously.  
	•	Look at making things equitable in terms of supporting all the grades over the course of the year.  
	•	Gr8’s are also fundraising for their year-end trip with popcorn sales & smencils.  
	•	CARE days are the week before winter break for gr7/8’s. They rotate between different activities for the last block of each day that week. Put on by PE dept. Can bring in a donation for the activities and it goes to a charity.  
	•	We’ll end the year with a staff/students volleyball game on the last day before the break.  
	•	This week gr8’s are going to see the Eastwood Gala show.
