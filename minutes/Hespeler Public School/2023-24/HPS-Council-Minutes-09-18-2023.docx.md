Chromebooks  
	•	How are Chromebooks assigned? Karen: a ratio of students:chromebooks is set by the board. Note that the Chromebooks are still property of the school, not the student personally.  
	•	We can no longer buy Chromebooks from council funds.  
	•	All Chromebooks with a barrel charger were recalled by the board which included most of what we had. Unfortunately we did not get 1 for 1 back for what we returned (they have a 4-year lifespan).  
	•	Grade 6, 7 & 8 classes have a 1:1 ratio, grades 1-5 have a 2:1 ratio. We’re fortunate that gr1-2 did not need as many because they also have some iPads, so all of our gr5’s have 1:1 ratio as well.  
	•	We have a few extras in case of breakage. The broken one goes back to the board for repair. There are different policies for if it stops working vs. if it’s broken by a student.  
	•	We’re trying to move back from COVID policies and do some less online again. It’s good for students to learn hands on & on paper as well. The idea is to allow students to choose what is best for them for their learning. It’s about making options more open for different ways to learn.  
	•	How do we accommodate for accessibility needs? We provide devices as needed for students, that’s kept aside from the other ratio.
