## Bingo (Kristin)

Cambridge Gaming Association allows organizations/schools to apply to take receive funds from  
bingo & slot machines.  
We’d like to apply to be accepted as a member organization, they have about 18 schools already.  
Last year \$1.6 million was brought in by member organizations.  
If accepted, they will tell us how many assignments we’ll have each month. 2 people plus a backup  
person are needed for each assignment. They recommend a roster of at least 6-8 volunteers as you  
get penalized with less assignments if your volunteers don’t show up.  
A 2-hour assignment slot can bring in \$900-1100 for the organization with 1-2 assignments per  
month.  
If approved they give a list of open assignments, and we can choose the ones we want to do.  
We don’t handle money, we call out the bingo numbers of winners and help clean up at the end.  
Training is 20-minute video plus one shadow shift.  
There’s a list of easy things they ask for in recognition in return.  
Because there’s a penalty for no-shows, admin thinks we should recruit from council to start, and  
then council members could recruit a helper they know well. We just need to be careful because it’s  
hard to replace a person as you need the training.  
Kristin would organize volunteers and confirm/remind ahead of shifts.  
Tyler motioned for Kristin to put in the application to the Cambridge Gaming Association, Jill  
seconded, approved unanimously.
