## New Business/Fundraising

## Big Yellow Bag update - Emily

We’ve had 27 bags bought through our promo code so far. So \$270 raised (\$10 per bag). They  
usually issue cheques at the end of a “season”. They do send Emily reports periodically. Emily will  
find out when we can expect a cheque to be issued.  
The fundraiser runs the whole year so we can keep promoting it as a reminder that it’s ongoing.  
Runs until the end of 2024. Discuss in December if council wants to continue in the new calendar  
year.  
Now that we know how it works, it would be easy to continue.  
People who buy also get a discount, so it’s a win on both sides. It’s also a very easy fundraiser,  
almost no work on our part so it’s a good return that way.  
Tents - Sharon  
Had some tent issues at fun fest (could have used more), we also used our branded tents at track &  
field days (which were very rainy this year). When we bought them initially we weren’t sure how  
many to buy and we have used those tents a lot this year.  
Our tents are much larger and sturdier than the retail tents people personally buy.  
There’s no cost savings to not doing the branding, so Sharon got a quote on more.  
Each tent is ~\$1,100, Sharon proposed that we consider getting more of them (2 vs 4). This does fall  
under the shade category, and we also fundraise for other things to support the school.  
8-week lead time so we could have them for the fall if we order now.  
They can continue to be kept in Mr. G’s storage room to keep them safe & clean.  
Are they available for teachers to use? We haven’t done that yet, only for council events and track so  
far. Something we could do in the interim while we fundraise for the permanent shade structure.  
Note that it’s a 4-person job to set them up and take them down. Could certainly be used for class  
time, but not for kids to play under/around.  
Jill motioned to purchase 4 tents (approx. \$5200), Crystal seconded, approved unanimously.  
Will be purchased from the WEFI account.  
Chocolate Bars - Sharon  
Deciding between the same \$5 bars (Laura Secord) as last year or chocolate sticks (World’s Finest)  
that are 3/\$5.  
Last year we made \$19,275 - it was a lot of work but it raised a lot of money, and it’s only one week.  
They can no longer take back open boxes. Most families sell their full box, but we could also sell  
open box leftovers at other events so that people aren’t committed to paying for the whole box.  
\$50/box profit on bars, profit is slightly less on sticks. Rep has had a lot of success with the sticks,  
you have to make less sales per person with selling 3 sticks at a time.  
The school didn’t do a fundraiser last year, they normally do. There will also be a school fundraiser  
next year as the school funds need replenishing to subsidize things for students. Steph suggested  
mint smoothies if council doesn’t do chocolate. Could be other things.  
Doing this would give us the rest of the funds for the shade structure quickly in the fall. Could run  
the sales period over Thanksgiving. Maybe school fundraiser could be around Christmas?  
Rep suggested switching to the sticks to change it up for people buying.  
Chocolate is sometimes overused because a lot of organizations sell them. But this is also true of  
many other fundraiser items.  
We likely can then bow back out of the fall fundraiser and help the school with theirs instead. We  
traditionally haven’t done a fall fundraiser up until last year when Karen & Rebecca couldn’t  
manage it through the office.  
Jesse motioned to do the chocolate sticks fundraiser, Kristin seconded, Emily voted nay, approved.
