## Administrators’ Update

● PD Day: Oct 11, 2024  
o Staff focusing on Universal Design, Behaviour Management Strategies,  
Reading/Writing support for students  
● EQAO test results are in.  
o Media reports don’t take into account the population and demographics of  
the school.  
o Our score does not have any effect on funding. We do receive the  
additional support of a Math Facilitator who comes for a number of weeks  
to work with students and staff.  

## Breakfast Program Update

o Currently have a snack bin offering at 2nd nutrition break (cheese,  
crackers, etc.)  
o Want to launch breakfast program and have volunteers ready to go to help  
run it, we’re currently waiting for updates on funding and timing from the  
Nutrition for Learning Program.  
● Courtyard Upgrades completed.  
o This project was mostly gifted as part of Pioneer Craftsman’s “Give Back”  
Project.  
o The entire school has been using the courtyard! It’s been great to see  
students out there doing work or simply enjoying the space.  
o Official ribbon cutting ceremony was held last week to thank the  
businesses who helped the project come to life. There is a plan in place to  
create a plaque to acknowledge donors.
