School council spending over $3,000

Expenses of $3,000 or more that WRDSB elementary school councils approved, spent, or seriously discussed, found by reading the council minutes downloaded from school websites (2018–2026). Playgrounds, outdoor learning spaces and Kindergarten areas are highlighted in green. Each entry links to every set of minutes that mentions it.

How to read this. "Amount" is the figure in the minutes: an approved budget, a quote, an amount spent, or a fundraising goal (see the note under the amount). Status reflects the latest mention found: Proposed / Requested / Under discussion (not yet decided), Approved / Earmarked (council voted or set money aside), Spent / Completed (paid or installed), Planning / Fundraising (multi-year projects). Board-funded items are included only where they shape council plans.

Limits. Only minutes the school posted online were checked (see minutes/manifest.csv); some years and schools are missing, and scanned documents were read with OCR. Amounts were found by searching for dollar figures of $1,000+ (then filtered to $3,000+), so a decision recorded without a dollar figure may be missed. Always confirm details in the linked minutes.

"Local copy" links open the downloaded file in this folder; "original" links go to the school website or Google Drive. Generated by build_expenses.py from data/expenses.jsonl.
