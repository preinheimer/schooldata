- **French cultural event grant:** Grant for French cultural events such as Carnaval; the minutes don't name the funder.

## What's included

- **Included:** money or goods from an outside organization (company, charity, service club, church, community group or government program) that the minutes say was received, approved or pledged.
- **Not included:**
  - Grants that were only applied for, or whose outcome isn't recorded (for example Kal Tire's Replay Fund, TD Friends of the Environment, Kitchener's Love My Hood).
  - Grants that were turned down or returned.
  - Donations from individual parents.
  - Large donations where the minutes don't say who gave them.
  - The $500 Parent Involvement (PIC) funding the Ministry gives every school automatically.
  - Money schools raised for other charities, and percentage-back fundraisers run by businesses.
  - Nutrition for Learning's regular budget for each school's snack program. It sets that budget by school size, and councils raise money *for* it rather than receiving grants from it.
- **Coverage:** only schools that post minutes online, and only what the minutes record. Councils often discuss grants without noting the result, so this list undercounts.
- **Accuracy:** entries were found by searching the minutes text and reviewed by hand. Some minutes say only "the grant"; those entries say so in *Used for*. Check the linked minutes before relying on an amount.
